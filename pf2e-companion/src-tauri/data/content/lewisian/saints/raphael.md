# Saint Raphael the Archangel

The angel of Tobit — companion on the road, healer of blindness, binder of
Asmodeus. Raphael's name means "God heals." He is canonical for Catholic
and Orthodox traditions (Tobit is in the deuterocanon); Protestant
tradition treats Tobit as supplementary but the figure of Raphael himself
is not theologically contested. The Lewisian lens includes him as
consensus-acceptable, with a note in the description that his attestation
is deuterocanonical for some traditions.

## Note for Reformed-leaning campaigns

If your table uses the strictly 66-book canon and prefers not to elevate
deuterocanonical figures, treat Raphael's appearances as edifying
literature rather than doctrine, or substitute "the angel of healing"
without a proper name. The mechanical role described below transfers
unchanged.

## Cleric / Champion patron

Cleric of Raphael chooses the **Healing** domain (primary). Champion
takes cause **Redemption** (redeemer) — Raphael's healing of Tobit's
blindness and Sarah's deliverance from Asmodeus map cleanly to the
redeemer's reactive aid.

- **Edicts (additional)**: tend to the sick and the wounded without
  partiality, accompany pilgrims on dangerous roads, bind demons that
  prey on the household.
- **Anathema (additional)**: refuse aid to a stranger, profit from
  another's affliction.

## Mechanical hooks

- *Heal* divine font is core. *Cleanse Affliction* (the Remaster rename
  of *Remove Disease*) is Raphael's signature ritual.
- *Banishment* against household-spirit antagonists matches the
  Asmodeus-binding trope.
