# Saint Uriel the Archangel

The angel of fire and revelation, named in 2 Esdras and central to the 1
Enoch tradition where he warns Noah of the coming flood and is set over
"the world and over Tartarus" (1 Enoch 20:2). Catholic devotion accepts
him; Orthodox tradition counts him among the seven archangels alongside
Selaphiel, Jegudiel, and Barachiel; Protestant tradition is largely silent
or skeptical because his attestation is deuterocanonical or non-canonical.

The Lewisian lens **includes Uriel with a flag**: this entry is the
"swing case" of [[lewisian:yhwh]]'s archangel roster. A campaign whose
table prefers a strictly canonical archangel set may omit Uriel without
breaking anything else in this pack.

## Cleric / Oracle patron

Cleric of Uriel chooses the **Fire** or **Truth** domain. Oracle takes
the **Cosmos** or **Battle** mystery (the warning-of-judgment + flame-
of-revelation combination is a strong cursebound fit — see
[[lewisian:class-oracle]]).

- **Edicts (additional)**: warn the unwary of coming judgment, illumine
  what is hidden, witness against falsehood.
- **Anathema (additional)**: hide a true vision, profit from the
  ignorance of another, allow a covenant to be desecrated unchecked.

## Mechanical hooks

- *Holy Light* and *Sunburst* are Uriel's flame on the page.
- *Foresight* is the canonical Uriel-aspected vision spell.
