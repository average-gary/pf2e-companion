# Saint Gabriel the Archangel

The herald who interprets visions to Daniel and announces the conceptions
of John the Baptist and Christ. Across all historic traditions Gabriel is
the angel of annunciation — the courier of meaning, of news, of summons.
Gabriel's voice initiates: he does not lead armies, he changes worlds with
words.

## Champion / Cleric patron

Strong Cleric or Oracle resonance; Champion fit is weaker since Gabriel is
a messenger, not a warrior. A Cleric of Gabriel chooses the **Knowledge**
or **Truth** domain; an Oracle takes the **Cosmos** or **Lore** mystery
(reframed as "the cosmic Word announcing the times").

- **Edicts (additional)**: deliver true news at any cost, interpret
  visions and dreams faithfully, refuse to be a herald of comfortable
  lies.
- **Anathema (additional)**: speak as one who knows when one does not,
  silence a prophet, falsify a message.

## Mechanical hooks

- Bard composition spells reskin as "Gabriel's annunciation" — courageous
  anthem becomes the herald's call.
- Sending and Augury at-will-flavor for very high-tier Gabriel-aspected
  servants.
