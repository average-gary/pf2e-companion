# Saint Michael the Archangel

The chief of the heavenly host. In Daniel he is "one of the chief princes"
who stands for the people of God; in Jude he disputes with the Devil over
the body of Moses; in Revelation he leads the armies of heaven against the
dragon. The Lewisian lens treats him as the consensus archangel — named
across all historic traditions, the patron of warriors, of angelic warfare,
and of those who guard the threshold between heaven and earth.

## Champion patron

A Champion who takes Michael as patron has cause **Justice** (paladin) or
**Liberation** (liberator). Suggested edicts/anathema overlay on the
[[lewisian:yhwh]] base:

- **Edicts (additional)**: contend against evil powers in the heavens and
  on earth, defend the people of God, refuse the dragon's contracts.
- **Anathema (additional)**: cede ground to demons by silence or fear,
  break ranks with one's brothers in arms.

## Mechanical hooks

- Champion's Aura takes a martial valence. The Sense Unholiness feat
  resonates with the dragon-trampling tradition (Rev 12:7).
- Holy Light (champion feat) reskinned as "Michael's spear" for flavor.
