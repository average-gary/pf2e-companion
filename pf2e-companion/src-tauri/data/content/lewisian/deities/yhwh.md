# YHWH — the Lord, I AM

The one God of historic Christian confession: maker of heaven and earth,
covenant-keeper, the I AM of Exodus 3, the Father almighty whose Word became
flesh and whose Spirit rests upon his people. The Lewisian lens defers
specifics that divide the historic traditions (sacramental theology, Marian
devotion, cessation/continuation of charisms) to the campaign's setting bible
and renders the deity entry at the level Lewis called "the hallway, not the
room" (*Mere Christianity*, preface).

## Stat block

- **Edicts**: love God with all your heart, love your neighbor as yourself,
  do justice and walk humbly, proclaim the gospel
- **Anathema**: take innocent life, swear false oaths in his name,
  consult mediums or spiritists, despair of his mercy
- **Areas of Concern**: covenant, creation, justice, mercy, the Word
- **Divine Attribute**: Wisdom or Charisma
- **Divine Font**: heal
- **Sanctification**: can choose holy
- **Divine Skill**: Religion
- **Favored Weapon**: longsword (the "sword of the Spirit" — Eph 6:17,
  Heb 4:12)
- **Domains**: Creation, Truth, Healing, Zeal
- **Alternate Domains**: Family, Knowledge, Soul, Vigil
- **Cleric Spells**: 1st *bless*; 3rd *holy light*; 5th *angelic messenger*
- **Religious Symbol**: a plain cross
- **Sacred Animal**: lamb
- **Sacred Colors**: gold and white

## Magic-theology lens

This lens assumes the **Charism + Lewisian hybrid** described in the wiki:
Divine-tradition spells are charisms in the sense of 1 Cor 12 (gift, not
technique); Arcane / Occult / Primal traditions are Lewisian conduits where
the player chooses the source — the faithful Watchers who remained obedient
to the Logos, or the fallen Watchers (1 Enoch 6–8) who taught humanity
forbidden arts. The line between licit and illicit is the line Aquinas
draws in Summa II-II q.96 a.2 (causal vs significatory) and Tolkien draws
in Letter 155 ("the supremely bad motive is domination of other free
wills"): an art is licit when it reads natural causation God built in or
is received as a charism in petition, illicit when it is technologized via
pact with fallen powers.

## Pharasma escape hatch

A campaign that prefers not to mechanize divine sanctity at all may set
sanctification to **none** (a real Paizo precedent — Pharasma, *Lost Omens:
Divine Mysteries* 2024). Christian-themed Champions then attach to a saint,
an archangel, or "the cause of righteousness" abstracted, rather than to
YHWH directly.
