# the New Jerusalem — the eschaton

The city descending out of heaven from God; the new heavens and new
earth in which righteousness dwells. Heaven and earth are not destroyed
and replaced; they are renewed, joined, and the dwelling of God is with
mortals. The lake of fire receives Death and Hades themselves
([[lewisian:cosmology-gehenna]]).

## PF2e plane mapping

There is no vanilla PF2e plane that maps cleanly. **The New Jerusalem
is a campaign endgame state** — the post-eschaton fusion of the
Material Plane with [[lewisian:cosmology-heaven]]. A 20+ level
adventure path that ends with the descent of the city (or with the
players present at the marriage supper of the Lamb) is the natural
shape of a Lewisian campaign's capstone.

## Mechanical notes

- Within the New Jerusalem, *Heal* is at-will and *Resurrect* is
  unnecessary — there is no death.
- The river of life and the tree of life are encounter-tier loot for a
  capstone scene, not items to grind for. Grant them as narrative
  resolution, not commodities.
- **There is no temple** (Rev 21:22) — the Lord God Almighty and the
  Lamb are its temple. This is a meaningful break with vanilla PF2e
  high-level cosmology and worth signposting in setting bibles.
