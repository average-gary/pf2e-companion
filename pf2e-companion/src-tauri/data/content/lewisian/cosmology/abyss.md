# the Abyss / *tehom* — the deep

The chaos-deep over which the Spirit hovered in Genesis 1:2, present
before the ordering of creation. In Revelation it is opened by a key, it
loses locusts, it swallows the dragon for a thousand years. Distinct
from final Hell ([[lewisian:cosmology-gehenna]]) and distinct from the
specific pit of bound Watchers ([[lewisian:cosmology-tartarus]]); the
Abyss is **the wider primordial deep**, both prison and unmade-place.

## PF2e plane mapping

Maps to the **Abyss** in vanilla PF2e nomenclature. In the Christian
double-origin tradition for fiends:

- **Devils** = Lucifer-fall hierarchy → courts of [[lewisian:cosmology-gehenna]]
- **Demons** = Watcher-fall hierarchy → ride out from the Abyss; the
  [[lewisian:cosmology-tartarus]] pit holds the named ringleaders;
  lesser shades roam the wider Abyss.
- **Daemons** = Revelation horsemen / personifications of plague,
  famine, death → lurk along the Abyss's edges.

## Campaign hooks

- Descents to the Abyss are *not* descents to final Hell. The water
  imagery of the deep (Job's Leviathan; the dragon-that-was-Pharaoh of
  Ezekiel 32) makes the Abyss a maritime-dungeon flavor distinct from
  Gehenna's furnace.
- The dragon's binding for a thousand years (Revelation 20) is a
  campaign-scale hook — it ends.
