# Gehenna — the lake of fire

The Valley of Hinnom outside Jerusalem; the rubbish-and-corpse fire that
became the New Testament's name for the eschatological place of
punishment. The lake of fire of Revelation 20–21. Final-state, not
intermediate; reserved for the unrepentant and for the dragon, the beast,
the false prophet, Death and Hades themselves.

## PF2e plane mapping

Maps to **Hell** (the lawful evil layer in vanilla PF2e nomenclature).
The Lewisian lens does not require commitment to a "lawful" character —
Gehenna is **lawful in the sense of being a verdict rendered**, not in
the sense of an alignment grid. Pride-fall fiends (the
[[lewisian:cosmology-abyss]]'s Lucifer-line devils, in the Christian
double-origin tradition) populate Gehenna's courts and prisons.

## Mechanical notes

- Sanctification of inhabitants: nearly all unholy.
- *Banishment* sends infernal creatures here as their original plane.
- An adventurer crossing Gehenna alive is doing harrowing-of-hell work;
  see Christ's descent in [[lewisian:cosmology-sheol]] for the canonical
  pattern.
