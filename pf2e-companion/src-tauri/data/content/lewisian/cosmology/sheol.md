# Sheol / Hades — the abode of the dead

The morally-neutral OT abode of the dead, rendered Hades by the LXX and
the New Testament. In Luke 16 it has compartments — a place of comfort
("Abraham's bosom") and a place of torment, separated by a great chasm.
1 Peter 3 has Christ preaching to "spirits in prison" between the
crucifixion and the resurrection.

## PF2e plane mapping

Maps to the **Boneyard** ferry zone — the pre-judgment intermediate
state where souls await sorting. **Distinct from final Hell**
([[lewisian:cosmology-gehenna]]); distinct from the **Abyss**
([[lewisian:cosmology-abyss]]) which is reserved for fallen angels and
chaos-spirits. Pharasma's cosmological role in vanilla PF2e (gatekeeper
of the dead) reskins cleanly onto Sheol — the gatekeeping function is
the same; the framework is monotheistic (the gatekeeping is delegated
authority, not autonomous deity).

## Campaign hooks

- A Cleric who descends to Sheol to harrow it is treading the road of
  Christ between Friday and Sunday.
- *Raise Dead* and *Resurrect* rituals reach into Sheol, not Heaven.
  This is theologically tighter than the vanilla framing and is worth
  noting in the lore as a moral weight on the spell — every resurrection
  is a soul recalled from intermediate rest.
