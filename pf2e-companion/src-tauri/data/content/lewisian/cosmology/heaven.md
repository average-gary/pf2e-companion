# Heaven — the Throne Room, the Third Heaven

Where the throne is. The throne room of Revelation 4–5; the third heaven
to which Paul was caught up (2 Cor 12:2); the sea of glass before the
throne; the four living creatures and the four-and-twenty elders. Not a
generic "good plane." This is the actual seat of the throne and the
courts surrounding it.

## PF2e plane mapping

Maps to the **Empyrean** / Heaven plane — the upper celestial reaches.
Reskin Paizo's celestial creature ladder onto the Pseudo-Dionysian
nine-rank hierarchy as documented in
[[lewisian:cosmology-angelic-hierarchy]]: Solar/Empyrean for Seraphim
and Cherubim; Planetar/Astral Deva for the governance triad
(Dominions/Virtues/Powers); guardian angels for the engaged triad
(Principalities/Archangels/Angels).

Per [[lewisian:yhwh]] anathema, **necromantic and consult-the-dead spells
do not function here** — the dead in Christ are not on call to mortal
casters. Necromancy in the heavenly courts requires a saint or martyr's
explicit intercession (a campaign hook, not a routine spell).
