# Tartarus — the prison of the fallen Watchers

A specific place of binding referenced once in canonical Scripture
(2 Peter 2:4): the deepest pit, where rebellious angels are "kept in
chains under darkness for the judgment of the great day." Jude 6 pairs
with this passage. The 1 Enoch tradition (non-canonical to most
Christians, canonical to Ethiopian Orthodox) names the bound angels —
Azazel under jagged rocks in Dudâêl, Semjâzâ and his hosts under the
hills for seventy generations.

## PF2e plane mapping

Maps to the **Tartarus floor of the Abyss** (or to a sealed sub-layer of
Hell, depending on the campaign's geography). **Distinct from
[[lewisian:cosmology-gehenna]]** — Gehenna is the lake-of-fire final
state of the unrepentant; Tartarus is the present-age binding of
specific named angelic offenders. Distinct from the broader
[[lewisian:cosmology-abyss]] / *tehom* of Genesis 1:2 and Revelation 9
which is a wider chaos-prison.

## Campaign hooks

- A high-level dungeon descent to recover a pre-Flood relic (a fragment
  of forbidden craft per 1 Enoch 8) is a classic Tartarus expedition.
- The forbidden-arts catalogue ([[lewisian:magic-forbidden-arts]] —
  pending) is the Watchers' teaching; PCs who recover it must decide
  whether to destroy, study, or wield it.
