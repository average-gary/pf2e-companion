# Oracle — the Old Testament prophet

A surprise win. The PF2e Remaster Oracle's **cursebound** mechanic —
power that comes with progressively worsening physical or social cost —
is structurally identical to the OT prophet experience.

> "If I say, 'I will not mention him, or speak any more in his name,'
> there is in my heart as it were a burning fire shut up in my bones,
> and I am weary with holding it in." — Jeremiah 20:9

Hosea's marriage. Ezekiel's silence and sign-acts. The Oracle's
mystery → the prophet's calling.

## Build recipe

1. **Mystery** (reflavor as "calling"):
   - **Lore** mystery → scribal prophet (Daniel, Ezra)
   - **Battle** mystery → herald of judgment (Elijah on Carmel,
     Jeremiah's siege oracles)
   - **Cosmos** mystery → apocalyptic visionary (Daniel 7-12,
     Ezekiel 1, 2 Esdras 13)
   - **Tempest** mystery → storm-and-fire prophet (Elijah 1 Kgs 17-19,
     2 Kgs 2)
   - **Life** mystery → resurrection-and-revival prophet (Elijah of
     widow's son; Elisha of Naaman)
2. **Curse**: render as the prophetic burden — physical signs that
   intensify with use. Hosea's marriage, Ezekiel's silence, Jeremiah's
   weeping.
3. **Deity**: [[lewisian:yhwh]] (and explicitly NOT a "mystery
   patron" in the polytheistic sense — the mystery is your calling,
   not a deity slot).

## Honest gotchas

- **No rules change needed.** This is the cleanest reskin in the entire
  pack. The Remaster's mystery → calling reframe means there's no
  polytheistic deity slot left to wrestle with.
- **The prophetic burden is a feature, not a punishment.** Players
  should be opting into the cursebound trade-off knowingly. If your
  table doesn't like that mechanic, the Oracle isn't the right class
  for the prophet flavor — try [[lewisian:class-cleric]] cloistered.
