# Cleric — priest, Levite, prophet

The PF2e Remaster Cleric reflavors cleanly: doctrine + edicts/anathema
framework is already deity-customizable, so a Christian Cleric is a
priest, Levite, prophet, or pastor depending on the campaign's tone.

## Build recipe

1. **Deity**: [[lewisian:yhwh]].
2. **Doctrine**:
   - **Cloistered cleric** = monastic / contemplative / scribal
     (Levite of Numbers; Daniel; the Beatitudes-school cleric).
   - **Warpriest** = chaplain to a Champion-led order, OR an OT
     priest-king like Melchizedek.
3. **Divine Font**: **heal** (universally for canonical clergy; *harm*
   is reserved for explicitly fallen / apostate clerics — see
   [[lewisian:magic-theology-approaches]]).
4. **Anathema**: *void-trait spells* are anathema for canonical clergy
   (replaces the "necromancy school is anathema" hook from
   pre-Remaster PF2e).

## Honest gotchas

- **Charism vs technique**. The DragonRaid trap is making piety a
  mechanical input. **Do not require the player to recite real
  scripture or pray to cast spells.** The mechanical surface is the
  same as any PF2e Cleric; the *flavor* is "the gift of healing,"
  "the word of knowledge," the charisms of 1 Cor 12.
- **Cessationist tables**. If your campaign's lens shifts toward
  Reformed sensibilities ([[lewisian:yhwh]] is consensus-aimed but
  swappable), the Cleric is still playable — frame each casting as a
  rare-providential sign rather than a routine resource. This is a
  fluff change, not a rule change.

## Domain choices for YHWH-aligned Clerics

Primary: Creation, Truth, Healing, Zeal. Alternate: Family, Knowledge,
Soul, Vigil. (These are the [[lewisian:yhwh]] domain set; substitute or
narrow for saint-patron Clerics — Raphael→Healing primary;
Michael→Zeal+Vigil primary.)
