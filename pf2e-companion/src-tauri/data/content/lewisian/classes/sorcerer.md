# Sorcerer — the Angelic bloodline canonical

The Sorcerer is the magic-saturated class with the cleanest reskin
**only because the Remaster shipped an Angelic bloodline as core**: it
grants the Divine tradition with explicit "descended from celestials"
flavor. That's the canonical Christian Sorcerer.

## Bloodline verdict table

| Bloodline | Verdict | Reskin |
|---|---|---|
| **Angelic** | **Ship — canonical** | Descended from a faithful Watcher who never fell; or from a saint's lineage; or from a unique calling like Jeremiah's "before I formed you in the womb I knew you" |
| Aberrant | Reframe → villain-leaning | Watcher-corrupted lineage; play as tragic-arc redemption |
| Imperial | Ship | Royal Davidic line; messianic ancestry as power |
| Wyrmblood | Ship (cautious) | Leviathan / Behemoth descent from Job 40-41 |
| Genie | Ship | Faithful elemental Watchers (the obedient remainder of the original 200 from 1 Enoch 6) |
| Psychopomp | Ship | Death-attendant angels of Sheol and the Boneyard ([[lewisian:cosmology-sheol]]) |
| **Demonic** | Cut or villain-only | Descent from a fallen Watcher (Azazel-line). Player-side only as tragic arc. |
| **Diabolic** | Cut or villain-only | Descent from the Lucifer-fall hierarchy. Same. |
| **Hag** | Cut | Mediumistic / Endor-line. Deut 18 forbidden territory. |
| **Shadow** | Cut | Sheol-haunted but theologically loaded; better as NPC. |
| **Undead** | Cut | Hard cut — `void` trait is anathema for canonical clergy and lineages. |

## Magic-theology framing (Charism + Lewisian hybrid)

The hybrid framework treats Arcane / Occult / Primal traditions as
Lewisian conduits where the **player chooses the source**. The Angelic
sorcerer draws on the obedient-Watcher remainder; the redeemed
Aberrant sorcerer wrestles with a corrupted lineage; the Demonic /
Diabolic NPC sorcerer is the antagonist. This **keeps every Sorcerer
build playable while giving casting moral stakes** — the line is
Tolkien's Letter 155: "the supremely bad motive is domination of other
'free' wills."

## Honest gotchas

- **Don't moralize at the table.** A Christian-themed game in which the
  Sorcerer is constantly being lectured isn't fun. Set the lens, then
  let players inhabit their characters.
- **The Demonic / Diabolic bloodlines as tragic-arc PCs** are the
  hardest framing and require GM buy-in. If your table isn't ready for
  that weight, just cut them.
