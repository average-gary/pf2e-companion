# Champion — the Lewisian Champion

The Remaster Champion is the single biggest mechanical reskin **win**
in PF2e for Christian-themed play. Sanctification (holy / unholy / none)
has replaced the alignment-locked causes; cause is now decoupled from
the four-axis grid; edicts/anathema attach to the deity. **Total
custom-rule footprint for a Christian Champion: zero.** Everything is
flavor.

## Build recipe

1. **Deity**: [[lewisian:yhwh]] (or, at the strict-monotheism extreme,
   `sanctification: none` with the cause attached to a saint or to
   "the cause of righteousness" in the abstract).
2. **Sanctification**: holy.
3. **Cause** = **Beatitude** flavor:
   - **Justice** (paladin) — Matthew 5:6, hunger and thirst for
     righteousness
   - **Liberation** (liberator) — Luke 4:18, proclaim freedom for the
     captives
   - **Mercy/Redemption** (redeemer) — Matthew 5:7, blessed are the
     merciful
4. **Edicts/anathema**: lift verbatim from [[lewisian:yhwh]] plus a
   saint patron's overlay if applicable ([[lewisian:michael]] for
   warriors of justice; [[lewisian:raphael]] for redeemer-healers).

## Honest gotchas

- **The Champion's Aura** is mechanically clean but flavor-loaded.
  Recommend describing it as the "Spirit's anointing" or "the
  Glory" — both are biblically resonant terms that land for both
  Reformed and charismatic players.
- **Sense Holiness / Sense Unholiness** feats reskin trivially as
  spiritual discernment (1 Cor 12:10).
- The **Holy / Unholy damage trait** is a real damage type now, not a
  cosmetic alignment marker. A Lewisian campaign's NPCs should respect
  this — devils and demons take holy; angels take unholy.

## Saint-patron variants

- **Patron Michael** (warrior): cause Justice/Liberation; edict overlay
  per [[lewisian:michael]].
- **Patron Raphael** (healer-redeemer): cause Redemption; edict
  overlay per [[lewisian:raphael]].
- **Patron Gabriel** (herald): cause Justice (proclamation-flavored);
  per [[lewisian:gabriel]].
