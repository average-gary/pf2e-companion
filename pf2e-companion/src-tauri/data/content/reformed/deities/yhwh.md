# YHWH — the Lord, the I AM

The one God of Christian Scripture: maker of heaven and earth, the I AM
of Exodus 3, sovereign in all things, covenant-keeper through Adam,
Noah, Abraham, Moses, David, and the New Covenant in Christ (see
[[reformed:covenants]]). The Reformed lens reads him through the
Westminster and Heidelberg confessions: revealed sufficiently and only
in the 66-book Protestant canon (sola scriptura), worshipped without
images (the regulative principle of worship), known as he chooses to
make himself known and not as we choose to imagine him.

His characteristic instrument in this lens is **the Word** — the
preached Word, the written Word, and Christ the Logos. Hebrews 4:12
("the word of God is living and active, sharper than any two-edged
sword") and Ephesians 6:17 ("the sword of the Spirit, which is the word
of God") give the canonical weapon imagery; the favored weapon below is
named for that text. Sacred colors are black and white — the
Reformation pulpit gown, the geneva bands, the unornamented sanctuary —
which is itself a confession against the visual maximalism that earlier
traditions deploy.

## Stat block

- **Edicts**: preach the Word in season and out (2 Tim 4:2), love God
  and neighbor (Mark 12:30-31), do justice and walk humbly (Mic 6:8),
  uphold the covenant in family and congregation
- **Anathema**: worship images or doctrines as idols (Ex 20:4-6),
  oppress the poor or the alien, presume upon God's mercy or despair of
  it, consult mediums or spiritists (Deut 18:10-12)
- **Areas of Concern**: the Word, covenant, justice, grace, providence
- **Divine Attribute**: Wisdom (with Charisma alternate)
- **Divine Font**: heal — used **sparingly**, in the
  minister-of-the-Word emphasis. See cessationism note below and
  [[reformed:class-cleric]].
- **Sanctification**: can choose holy (or **none** — the Pharasma
  escape hatch from the YHWH template; see [[reformed:class-sorcerer]]
  and the note below)
- **Divine Skill**: Religion
- **Favored Weapon**: longsword (the "sword of the Spirit" — Eph 6:17;
  cf. the two-edged sword from Christ's mouth in Rev 1:16, 19:15)
- **Domains**: Truth, Knowledge, Zeal, Vigil
- **Alternate Domains**: Creation, Healing, Soul
- **Cleric Spells**: 1st *bless*; 3rd *holy light*; 5th *command* (the
  Word that orders creation — Gen 1; Ps 33:9)
- **Religious Symbol**: an open Bible above a plain cross
- **Sacred Animal**: lamb
- **Sacred Colors**: black and white (the Reformation pulpit gown)

## Cessationism caveat (load-bearing)

The Reformed tradition holds that the **apostolic charisms** (tongues,
prophecy, miraculous healing, sign-miracles) **ceased with the closing
of the canon** — they authenticated the apostles and the deposit of
faith, and are not the ordinary furniture of the post-apostolic church
(B.B. Warfield, *Counterfeit Miracles*; the Westminster tradition
broadly). This makes routine PF2e Cleric casting theologically loaded
in this lens. The canonical move in this pack is to **frame each
casting as a rare-providential sign** rather than a routine class
resource — flavor change, not rules change. See
[[reformed:class-cleric]] for the full treatment, including the
cessationist-table houserule that limits Cleric to NPC-only or
biblical-figure-only PCs.

## Pharasma escape hatch

The YHWH template documents `sanctification: none` (Pharasma's
*Lost Omens: Divine Mysteries* 2024 precedent) as a real Paizo-supported
shape. A Reformed table that finds the holy/unholy damage trait
theologically uncomfortable — mechanizing the divine name as a damage
type — can take this path. Champions then attach to **the cause of
righteousness** in the abstract (Justice / Mercy / Faithfulness / Truth)
rather than to YHWH-the-deity-with-sanctification.

## Saint-patrons (NOT here)

The Reformed tradition rejects the invocation of saints (Heidelberg
Catechism Q.94; Calvin, *Institutes* III.20.21-27). The exemplary
biblical witnesses in this pack ([[reformed:michael]],
[[reformed:gabriel]], [[reformed:abraham]], [[reformed:moses]],
[[reformed:david]], [[reformed:paul]]) are **examples of faith** in the
Hebrews 11 sense, **not patrons** to whom Champions or Clerics dedicate
their service. Champion causes attach to abstract virtues, not to
these figures. Be explicit about this with your table.
