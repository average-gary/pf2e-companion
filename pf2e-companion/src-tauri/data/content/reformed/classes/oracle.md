# Oracle — the Old Testament prophet

The Oracle's **cursebound** mechanic — power that comes with
progressively worsening physical or social cost — is structurally
identical to the OT prophet experience. Jeremiah 20:9, Hosea's
marriage, Ezekiel's silence and sign-acts, Daniel's exhaustion
(Dan 8:27), Isaiah walking naked three years (Isa 20:3). The Remaster's
**mystery → calling** reframe makes this the cleanest reskin in the
pack.

## The cessation caveat (Oracle-specific)

Reformed cessationism applies as much to **prophecy** as to other
charisms — perhaps more so, since the closing of the canon is
specifically "no new revelation." The Westminster Confession (I.1) is
load-bearing: God formerly revealed his will "at sundry times, and in
divers manners" but "those former ways of God's revealing his will
unto his people being now ceased" (cf. Heb 1:1-2 — God spoke
"at many times and in many ways" *but in these last days* has spoken
in his Son). The Oracle is therefore most comfortably an
**apostolic-or-pre-apostolic-age PC** in this lens — Jeremiah, Daniel,
Anna, Agabus.

For a **post-canon Reformed campaign**, the Oracle resolves one of
three ways:

1. **NPC-only**, parallel to the Cleric resolution (B) in
   [[reformed:class-cleric]]. Strictest cessationist solution.
2. **Reflavor as a "burdened preacher"** — the cursebound mechanic
   becomes the cost of faithful proclamation in a hostile age (cf.
   Bonhoeffer, the Covenanters). Powers narrated as
   discernment-and-conviction rather than as new revelation.
3. **Cut from the playable class set** entirely.

## Build recipe (under apostolic-age framing)

1. **Mystery** (reframe as "calling"):
   - **Lore** mystery → scribal prophet (Daniel, Ezra)
   - **Battle** mystery → herald of judgment (Elijah on Carmel,
     Jeremiah's siege oracles)
   - **Cosmos** mystery → apocalyptic visionary (Daniel 7-12,
     Ezekiel 1)
   - **Tempest** mystery → storm-and-fire prophet (Elijah 1 Kgs 17-19)
   - **Life** mystery → resurrection-prophet (Elijah / Elisha)
2. **Curse**: render as the prophetic burden.
3. **Deity**: [[reformed:yhwh]].

## Honest gotchas

- **The prophetic burden is a feature.** Players should be opting in
  with eyes open. Hosea's marriage and Ezekiel's silence are *not*
  cosmetic.
- **Don't import Catholic mystic flavor.** Reformed prophet-flavor is
  Word-mediated and covenant-bound, not infused-grace ecstatic.
  Closer to Knox than to Teresa of Ávila.
