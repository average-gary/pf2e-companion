# Sorcerer — the heaviest Reformed cuts

In a continuationist or Lewisian lens the Sorcerer's **Angelic
bloodline** is the canonical Christian-themed sorcerer. **In the
Reformed lens, even Angelic strains.** Cessationism plus the
all-non-divine-is-demonic lean of [[magic-theology-approaches]]
approaches #3 and #4 cuts deeply here: Reformed theology is
**hostile to inherited supernatural power that bypasses the Word and
the Spirit's ordinary means** (preaching, sacraments, prayer).

## The Pharasma escape hatch (use this)

The cleanest Reformed-coherent path is to **`sanctification: none`** —
the YHWH template's documented Pharasma-precedent option (see
[[reformed:yhwh]]). Under this build:

- The Sorcerer's bloodline is a **lineage-flavored ancestry trait**,
  not a pact with celestial powers.
- The character has **no holy/unholy damage trait**, no automatic
  alignment-of-bloodline implication, no theological commitment to
  what celestial-or-fallen heritage *means*.
- Mechanically the spell list functions as RAW. Theologically the
  power is treated as a Romans-9 mystery — a providential allocation
  of natural ability that the character must use faithfully or
  unfaithfully.

This dodges the worst of the cessationist-vs-bloodline conflict.

## Bloodline verdict table (Reformed)

| Bloodline | Verdict | Reformed reasoning |
|---|---|---|
| **Angelic** | Reflavor as **Davidic / Imperial** lineage | Cessationism + flat angelology makes "descended from celestials" theologically loaded; royal-Davidic line ([[reformed:david]]) is the cleanest reskin |
| **Imperial** | **Ship** | Royal Davidic line; messianic ancestry as power |
| **Aberrant** | Cut | Watcher-corrupted lineage; under approach #3 this is fallen-angel-pact |
| **Wyrmblood** | Cut or cautious | Leviathan / Behemoth descent borders mythological speculation |
| **Genie** | Cut | Elemental-being lineage borders folk-magic / pact framing |
| **Psychopomp** | **Cut** | Death-attendant angels are speculative angelology Calvin specifically warns against |
| **Demonic** | **Hard cut** | Pact with fallen powers; no tragic-arc framing — Reformed theology has less room for "redeemable demonic lineage" than Catholic/Orthodox/Lewisian |
| **Diabolic** | **Hard cut** | Same |
| **Hag** | **Hard cut** | Mediumistic / Endor-line; Deut 18 forbidden |
| **Shadow** | **Hard cut** | Sheol-haunted; trespasses on closed intermediate state |
| **Undead** | **Hard cut** | Void-trait spells anathema |

The Reformed playable bloodline set is therefore narrow:
**Imperial only**, with Angelic reflavored to Imperial, and a
recommendation that even Imperial be played under
`sanctification: none`.

## If the table wants more bloodlines

Run [[magic-theology-approaches]] approach #2 (Lewisian) under the
Reformed lens — accepting that you've stepped slightly outside strict
Reformed orthodoxy in the direction of the broader Christian
imaginative tradition (Lewis was Anglican). This is a self-conscious
loosening. Document it as a setting variant for your table; do not
present it as Reformed-orthodox.

## Honest gotchas

- **Don't moralize at the table.** Even a Reformed-themed game in
  which the Sorcerer is constantly being lectured isn't fun. Set
  the lens, then let players play.
- **The Pharasma escape hatch is genuinely the most theologically
  rigorous AND the most mechanically restrictive option** — accept
  the trade.
