# Cleric — minister of the Word, with the cessationism caveat

The Reformed Cleric is **the hardest reskin in this lens**. The
Reformed tradition is **cessationist** (see the load-bearing wiki
article on cessationism vs continuationism, and the Reformed row of
[[reformed:yhwh]]'s cessationism caveat): the apostolic charisms —
tongues, prophecy, miraculous healing, sign-miracles — **ceased with
the closing of the canon** (Warfield, *Counterfeit Miracles*; Calvin,
*Institutes* IV.19.18 read against the broader Reformed reception).
PF2e Cleric assumes a continuationist baseline. The reskin must
reconcile this directly; this is non-negotiable in the lens.

## Three resolutions (pick one for your table)

### A. Frame as rare-providential signs (default for this pack)

Mechanically the same as PF2e RAW, but the **fluff is shifted**: every
casting is narrated as an *unusual* providential intervention rather
than a routine resource. The Cleric is a minister of the Word who
*sometimes* sees God answer prayer extraordinarily. This is the
softest cessationist reading and matches the lived practice of many
Reformed traditions ("God still does miracles, just not on demand").
**No rules change.** The player should narrate accordingly.

### B. Houserule: Cleric is NPC-only / biblical-figure-only

The strict-cessationist solution. The Cleric class is reserved for
**named biblical figures** (Elijah, Elisha, Moses, Paul) as one-off
NPC playables, or for **prophetic-age PCs** in a campaign set before
the canon's closing (a "second-temple" or "apostolic-age" campaign).
Post-canon PCs cannot take the Cleric class. This pushes the table
toward [[magic-theology-approaches]] approach #4 (strict-historicist).

### C. Lean toward approach #3 (all-non-divine-is-demonic)

If your table wants Cleric playable post-canon AND honors strong
cessationism, run [[magic-theology-approaches]] approach #3 — the
Cleric (and Champion / Oracle) are the **only legitimate casters**;
all other casting traditions are reframed as pact-magic with fallen
powers ([[reformed:cosmology-tartarus]], [[reformed:class-sorcerer]]).
This narrows the playable class set sharply.

## Build recipe (under resolution A)

1. **Deity**: [[reformed:yhwh]].
2. **Doctrine**:
   - **Cloistered cleric** = Bible-scholar / pastor-theologian (a
     Genevan academy resonance; Calvin in his pastoral office).
   - **Warpriest** = chaplain to a magistrate or covenant-army (Cromwell's
     New Model Army chaplains; Knox at the head of Scottish reformers).
3. **Divine Font**: **heal** (sparingly per the Reformed YHWH stat
   block; *harm* font is reserved for explicitly fallen / apostate
   clerics — antichrist NPC pattern only).
4. **Anathema**: *void-trait spells* are anathema for canonical
   clergy. **Necromancy is doubly anathema** under cessationism — it
   trespasses on the sealed Sheol that the elect have left
   ([[reformed:cosmology-sheol]]) and the unjudged dead awaiting
   judgment.
5. **Domains**: per [[reformed:yhwh]] — Truth, Knowledge, Zeal, Vigil
   (primary); Creation, Healing, Soul (alternate). **Note the
   primary set leans Word-and-witness over miracle-and-power** —
   this is the deliberate Reformed shape.

## DragonRaid trap (don't)

**Do not require the player to recite real scripture or pray to cast
spells.** This pack is an aid to imaginative play, not a piety
mechanic. The mechanical surface is RAW PF2e Cleric; the *flavor* is
"the gift of preaching faithfully," "the fruit of the Spirit," "an
extraordinary mercy on a particular day." A Reformed table should
not tolerate piety-as-mechanic; the prior-Christian-RPG-history wiki
section documents the DragonRaid failure mode at length.
