# Champion — the Reformed Champion

The Remaster Champion is the cleanest mechanical fit in PF2e for
Christian-themed play, and it survives the Reformed lens almost
intact — with **one specific structural change**: causes attach to
**abstract virtues**, not to named saints.

## The named-saint cut

In the Catholic and Orthodox lenses Champions can take a saint as
patron (St George, St Michael, St Raphael) — the cause is essentially
the saint's portfolio. The Reformed tradition rejects saint-veneration
explicitly (Heidelberg Q.94; Calvin, *Institutes* III.20.21-27): **no
prayer to saints, no patronal mediation, no relic-anchored cause**.
The covenant-figure entries in this pack ([[reformed:michael]],
[[reformed:gabriel]], [[reformed:abraham]], [[reformed:moses]],
[[reformed:david]], [[reformed:paul]]) are exemplary witnesses, **not
patrons**. Each of those entries restates this distinction explicitly.

## Build recipe

1. **Deity**: [[reformed:yhwh]] (or `sanctification: none` per the
   Pharasma escape hatch — the Champion's cause then attaches to
   "the cause of righteousness" abstracted, with no holy/unholy
   damage trait).
2. **Sanctification**: holy.
3. **Cause** = **abstract virtue under YHWH directly**:
   - **Justice** (paladin) — Mic 6:8, "do justice"
   - **Mercy/Redemption** (redeemer) — Matt 5:7, "blessed are the
     merciful"
   - **Liberation** (liberator) — Luke 4:18, "proclaim freedom for
     the captives"
   - **Faithfulness** (suggested fourth, Reformed-distinctive) —
     covenantal *hesed*; the long obedience in the same direction.
     Mechanically use Justice or Mercy chassis with covenant-flavor
     edicts.
   - **Truth** (suggested fifth) — Eph 6:14, the belt of truth.
     Reformed lenses lean on this one heavily.
4. **Edicts/anathema**: lift verbatim from [[reformed:yhwh]]; **do
   not overlay a saint-patron tier**.

## Word-as-sword imagery (Reformed-distinctive)

The Reformed canonical weapon-image is **the Word as sword** (Heb 4:12,
"the word of God is living and active, sharper than any two-edged
sword"; Eph 6:17, "the sword of the Spirit, which is the word of
God"). The favored weapon on the [[reformed:yhwh]] stat block
(longsword) lands here. **Lean into this in flavor**: a Reformed
Champion's blade is named for or inscribed with Scripture; the killing
blow is narrated as the Word piercing what it strikes.

## Honest gotchas

- **Champion's Aura** as the Spirit's anointing or the Glory — both
  scripturally clean. Avoid "halo" framing (Catholic-coded).
- **Sense Holiness / Sense Unholiness** = **discernment of spirits**
  (1 Cor 12:10) — but cessationist-flavored, narrated as moral
  discernment refined through Word-saturation rather than as a
  charismatic gift.
- **Holy / Unholy damage** is real damage in PF2e Remaster.
  Reformed tables that find this theologically uncomfortable should
  use the `sanctification: none` build — see [[reformed:yhwh]]'s
  Pharasma escape hatch.
