# David — covenant king, exemplary witness

The shepherd-king to whom YHWH promised an everlasting throne
(2 Sam 7:12-16); the type of Christ the messianic son of David
(Matt 1:1; Rom 1:3); the psalmist whose voice supplies the Reformed
church its **prayer book** (Calvin called the Psalter "an anatomy of
all the parts of the soul"). The Davidic covenant ([[reformed:covenants]])
is the messianic hinge between Mosaic and New: Christ rules from
David's throne.

David's **failure** is also instructive (2 Sam 11-12) — Reformed
preaching uses David's adultery, murder, and Psalm 51 repentance as the
canonical pattern of a saint who is simultaneously *iustus et peccator*
(Calvin, *Institutes* III.3-4 on repentance).

## Champion-cause distinction (read this)

David is the canonical Old-Testament exemplar of **Justice** under
YHWH (the kingly virtue of covenant administration) and a model of
public repentance, but he is **not** a Champion patron. A Reformed
table runs David's harp, sling, or psalter as cultural touchstones and
NPC-ancestor flavor; the Champion's dedication remains to YHWH and the
abstract virtue.
