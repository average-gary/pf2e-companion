# Michael the Archangel — exemplary witness

One of two named archangels in the Protestant 66-book canon (with
[[reformed:gabriel]]). In Daniel he is "one of the chief princes" who
stands for the people of God; in Jude he disputes with the devil over
the body of Moses; in Revelation 12 he leads the heavenly host against
the dragon. The Reformed lens treats him as a **named scriptural
witness** — present in the canon, available as setting flavor — but
**not as a patron** to whom Champions or Clerics dedicate themselves
(Heidelberg Q.94; Calvin, *Institutes* I.14.10-12 against speculative
angelology and against angel-veneration).

## Champion-cause distinction (read this)

A Reformed Champion does **not** take "Michael" as a patron. The
Champion cause is the **abstract virtue** — Justice, Faithfulness,
Truth — attached to YHWH directly. Michael appears in the campaign
**as an angel YHWH may send** (Heb 1:14, "ministering spirits"), not as
a deity-substitute or invocation target. The flavor of dragon-trampling
courage is available; the cult is not.
