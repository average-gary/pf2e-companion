# Abraham — covenant patriarch, exemplary witness

The father of the faithful (Rom 4:11). The Abrahamic covenant
([[reformed:covenants]]) is the load-bearing scaffold of Reformed
covenant theology: a free promise (Gen 15:6, "he believed the Lord, and
it was counted to him as righteousness"), sealed in circumcision (Gen
17), tested at Moriah (Gen 22). Reformed soteriology reads the whole
Bible as the unfolding of this covenant through Moses, David, and
Christ.

## Champion-cause distinction (read this)

Abraham is the canonical biblical example of **Faithfulness** as a
virtue, but a Reformed Champion does **not** take Abraham as patron.
The Champion's cause is Faithfulness-as-virtue under YHWH; Abraham
appears in the campaign as the historical-canonical exemplar (Heb 11),
not as a venerated saint. Use him as an NPC ancestor, a vision in the
heavenly courts (cf. Luke 16:22-31), or a name on a covenant-token
inheritance — never as the recipient of a Cleric's prayers.
