# Sheol / Hades — the abode of the dead

The Old Testament word for the grave / the realm of the dead, rendered
Hades in the Septuagint and the New Testament. In Reformed reading
this is **not an intermediate purgative state** for the righteous
(Westminster XXXII.1; Calvin *Institutes* III.5) — the righteous go
directly to glory at death. Sheol / Hades in this lens is therefore
**polemically narrowed**: the abode of the **unjudged wicked** awaiting
the resurrection-and-final-judgment of Revelation 20:11-15.

Luke 16's "bosom of Abraham" account is read as parable / pre-Calvary
narrative, not as a permanent post-resurrection cartography. Christ's
descent (1 Pet 3:19) is read variously across Reformed expositors — the
Heidelberg Catechism (Q.44) reads "he descended into hell" of the
Apostles' Creed as Christ's **suffering on the cross** (the descent of
soul-anguish), not a literal trip to a sub-plane.

## PF2e plane mapping

Maps to the **Boneyard ferry zone** as a constrained, judgment-awaiting
holding space — **not** a destination for player-character afterlife.
Pharasma's gatekeeper role reskins as a delegated angelic warden, not
as autonomous deity.

## No purgatory (flag this explicitly)

As with [[reformed:cosmology-heaven]], the absence of purgatory is
load-bearing in this lens. Calvin: "we must cry out with the shouting
not only of our voices but of our throats and lungs that purgatory is a
deadly fiction of Satan" (*Institutes* III.5.6). Reformed
worldbuilders should **not** build a transitional-plane subsystem here.
If a player wants ritual mechanics for "praying for the dead," that is
a Catholic/Orthodox lens setting and you should switch packs rather
than houserule it in.

## Campaign hooks

- *Raise Dead* and *Resurrect* are theologically loaded in this lens —
  for the elect, the soul has *already* gone to glory and would have
  to be recalled from the beatific vision (cf. 1 Sam 28:15, Samuel's
  "why have you disturbed me?"). Frame as rare-providential, not
  routine. Many Reformed tables simply cut these spells.
