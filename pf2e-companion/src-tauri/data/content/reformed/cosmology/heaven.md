# Heaven — the Throne Room, the Third Heaven

Where the throne is. The throne room of Revelation 4-5; the third
heaven to which Paul was caught up (2 Cor 12:2); the sea of glass
before the throne. Reformed eschatology emphasizes the **immediate
beatific vision**: the souls of the righteous, "being then made
perfect in holiness, are received into the highest heavens, where they
behold the face of God in light and glory" (Westminster Confession
XXXII.1). No detour, no waiting room.

## PF2e plane mapping

Maps to the **Empyrean / Heaven** plane in PF2e. The angelic hierarchy
populating it is **flat in this lens** — angel and archangel only, with
Michael and Gabriel as the only named archangels (Daniel, Jude,
Revelation 12, Luke 1). Solar / Empyrean / Planetar / cassisian-tier
distinctions can still be used for *power-tier* purposes, but **do not
import the Pseudo-Dionysian nine-rank scheme** as in-world canon —
Reformed theology is suspicious of speculative angelology beyond
Scripture (Calvin, *Institutes* I.14.4: "Let us remember here, as in
all religious doctrine, that we ought to hold to one rule of modesty
and sobriety: not to speak, or guess, or even to seek to know,
concerning obscure matters anything except what has been imparted to
us by God's Word").

## No purgatory (a polemical absence)

There is **no purgatory** in this cosmology. The Westminster
Confession (XXXII.1) names only two destinations after death: the
righteous to glory immediately, the wicked to hell. The Heidelberg
Catechism (Q.57) names "even now my soul, after this life, shall be
taken up to Christ my Head." Calvin in *Institutes* III.5 calls
purgatory "a deadly fiction of Satan" that "nullifies the cross of
Christ." This is the load-bearing polemical absence of the Reformed
lens; if your campaign needs a transitional plane, run [[reformed:cosmology-sheol]]
as the **intermediate state for the unjudged** (the wicked dead
awaiting final judgment), not as a purgative space for the righteous.
