# the New Jerusalem — the eschaton

The city descending out of heaven from God; the new heavens and new
earth in which righteousness dwells. Reformed eschatology is broadly
**amillennial or postmillennial** (the dispensational pre-millennial
reading is a 19th-century minority within the broader Reformed
tradition); both mainstream readings emphasize the **renewal of
creation**, not its annihilation. Romans 8:18-25 is load-bearing: "the
creation itself will be set free from its bondage to corruption."

The Westminster Confession (XXXIII.3) names the day of judgment as the
day "the wicked who know not God... shall be cast into eternal
torments... but the righteous, going into everlasting life, shall
receive that fullness of joy and refreshing which shall come from the
presence of the Lord." Two destinations, one judgment-day, no third
state.

## PF2e plane mapping

There is no vanilla PF2e plane that maps cleanly. **The New Jerusalem
is a campaign endgame state** — the post-eschaton fusion of the
Material Plane with [[reformed:cosmology-heaven]]. A 20+ level
adventure path that ends with the descent of the city is the natural
shape of a Reformed campaign's capstone. The Reformed flavor of this
endgame leans **Word-saturated**: Christ the Logos as the city's
light (Rev 21:23), the river of life *and* the open Bible-as-tree-of-
life (a reasonable visual conflation for a Reformed setting given the
Word-as-life imagery of John 6:63 and Heb 4:12).

## Mechanical notes

- *Heal* at-will inside the city; *Resurrect* unnecessary (no death).
- **No temple** (Rev 21:22) — God and the Lamb are its temple. The
  Reformed regulative principle of worship lands here especially
  cleanly: the worship of the new creation is the unmediated vision
  of God himself.
- The descent of the city is the resolution of the New Covenant
  ([[reformed:covenants]]) — the marriage of the Lamb to his bride,
  the visible church made universal.
