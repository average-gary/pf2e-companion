# The Six Covenants — Reformed worldbuilding scaffold

Reformed theology reads the whole Bible as **one unfolding covenant of
grace** administered through six successive historical
**administrations** (Westminster Confession VII; the Federal /
covenant-theology tradition of Cocceius, Witsius, and the Marrow Men).
This is the **load-bearing distinctive** of a Reformed campaign
setting — the Catholic / Orthodox lens emphasizes sacraments, the
Pentecostal lens emphasizes the Spirit, the Reformed lens emphasizes
the **covenantal architecture of history**. Use this as your
campaign's *time-axis* — different in-world ages live under different
covenant administrations.

## The six covenants

1. **Adamic / the covenant of works** (Gen 1-3). The pre-fall state
   of probationary obedience. *Worldbuilding:* a lost
   pre-historical age, before the Fall, sometimes recoverable as
   campaign backstory (Edenic remnants, [[reformed:cosmology-new-jerusalem]]
   as eschatological return). The Tree of Life is the canonical
   image.

2. **Noahic** (Gen 9:8-17). Covenant of common grace — the rainbow,
   the preservation of creation under judgment. **Universal in
   scope** (with all flesh, not just elect humanity). *Worldbuilding:*
   civic order, the legitimacy of magistracy, the stability of the
   natural world. The post-flood age in which most fantasy campaigns
   actually run.

3. **Abrahamic** (Gen 12, 15, 17). Covenant of grace formally
   inaugurated. **Free promise**: land, seed, blessing, sealed in
   circumcision, justified by faith (Gen 15:6 → Rom 4). *Worldbuilding:*
   the calling-out of a covenant people from among the nations; a
   chosen-people setting (cf. [[reformed:abraham]]). Pilgrim flavor:
   "looking for a city whose builder and maker is God" (Heb 11:10).

4. **Mosaic** (Exodus 19-24; Deuteronomy). Covenant of law — the
   Decalogue, the sacrificial system, the theocratic polity. Reformed
   theology distinguishes the **threefold use of the law** (Calvin,
   *Institutes* II.7.6-12): mirror (exposing sin), restraint (civil),
   guide (sanctification). *Worldbuilding:* a theocratic-age campaign,
   the giving of a written law-code to a wilderness people, the
   tabernacle as mobile-cosmos (cf. [[reformed:moses]]).

5. **Davidic** (2 Samuel 7:12-16). Covenant of kingship — an
   everlasting throne in David's line, fulfilled in Christ.
   *Worldbuilding:* a kingdom-and-temple age; the messianic line as
   campaign through-line; restoration politics in exile (cf.
   [[reformed:david]]). The temple-and-throne pairing is the political-
   theological heart of OT setting material.

6. **New Covenant** (Jeremiah 31:31-34; Luke 22:20; Heb 8-10).
   Covenant of grace consummated in Christ — the law written on the
   heart, the priesthood of all believers, the closed canon as the
   covenant document. **Reformed cessationism is grounded here**: the
   New Covenant's apostolic charisms authenticated the closing of the
   canon and ceased with it (see [[reformed:class-cleric]]).
   *Worldbuilding:* the present age, the church-as-pilgrim-people,
   awaiting the descent of [[reformed:cosmology-new-jerusalem]].

## Campaign-shape implications

- A **single-covenant campaign** runs cleanest — pick one of the six
  ages and stay there. Mosaic-age (Joshua/Judges-style) is naturally
  high-magic in flavor; New-Covenant-age leans cessationist-low-magic.
- A **multi-covenant campaign** uses time-travel, vision sequences, or
  multi-generational play to traverse covenant administrations. Higher
  ceiling, harder design.
- The **eschaton** ([[reformed:cosmology-new-jerusalem]]) is the
  resolution of *all* the covenants — the Adamic state restored, the
  Noahic creation renewed, the Abrahamic land universalized, the
  Mosaic law inscribed on every heart, the Davidic throne occupied by
  Christ, the New Covenant consummated.
