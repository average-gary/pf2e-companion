# Theosis — soteriology and the level-20 capstone

**The most distinctive Orthodox doctrine** and the throughline of this
content pack. Theosis (Greek *theosis* — "deification") is the Orthodox
account of salvation: not merely forensic justification, not merely
imputed righteousness, but progressive **participation in the divine
life** through the Holy Spirit. The scriptural anchor is **2 Peter 1:4**:
"that through these you may become **partakers of the divine nature**."

## The patristic chain

- **Athanasius**, *On the Incarnation* §54: *autos gar enenthropesen,
  hina hemeis theopoiethomen* — "for he became man, so that we might be
  made god." The Christological hinge: the Incarnation is the necessary
  condition for human deification.
- **Gregory of Nyssa**: *epektasis* — the perpetual ascent. Even in
  glory, the soul never exhausts the divine; the journey continues
  forever, each plateau opening on a deeper.
- **Maximus the Confessor**: the cosmos itself is on the becoming-divine
  trajectory through the incarnate Logos (*Ambigua* 7, 41). Creation is
  not abandoned; it is fulfilled.
- **Gregory Palamas** (14th century, defending the Athonite hesychasts
  against Barlaam): the **essence/energies distinction**. Humans
  participate in God's uncreated *energies* — operations, grace,
  uncreated light — but never in his *essence*. This is the conceptual
  hinge that makes deification coherent: creatures genuinely become
  partakers of the divine nature without ever becoming God. See
  [[orthodox:gregory-palamas]].
- **Symeon the New Theologian**, **Seraphim of Sarov**: the experiential
  attestation. Symeon describes being filled with uncreated light;
  Seraphim's conversation with Motovilov in the snow at Sarov is the
  most famous modern instance — the elder's face shining brighter than
  the sun, the disciple unable to look at him.

## The three stages

1. **Katharsis** (purification) — the work of repentance, fasting,
   confession, almsgiving, the keeping of the commandments. The body
   and soul are made clear. PC levels 1–7.
2. **Theoria** (illumination) — direct experiential knowledge of God;
   the prayer of the heart bears fruit; visions, prophetic charisms,
   the *gift of tears*. PC levels 8–14.
3. **Theosis** (union) — participation in the uncreated energies;
   the saint glows. PC levels 15–20.

## The level-20 capstone mechanic

A PC at level 20 — of any class, though most readable for
[[orthodox:class-cleric]] and [[orthodox:class-monk]] — may take a
**capstone feat-equivalent** named **Tabor Light**:

- The PC gains the *Light* cantrip at-will (no slot, no Focus cost).
- The PC manifests a **cosmetic glow** visible to any creature with
  spiritual sight — any celestial, any fiend, any creature using
  *Detect Magic*, *Read Aura*, *True Seeing*, or comparable. The glow
  is brighter when the PC is at peace and dims under wrath; this is
  flavor, not mechanism.
- **No damage trait. No save bonus. No healing pulse.** The glow is
  **fitness for the new creation** ([[orthodox:cosmology-new-jerusalem]])
  made visible. It is a narrative recognition that the PC's arc has
  completed; it is not a power-budget item.

The discipline matters: tables that mechanize the glow have
inadvertently re-collapsed essence and energies into a single
mechanical surface, which the Palamite tradition explicitly rejects.
The cosmetic-only constraint is theological, not balance-driven.

## On the Catholic / Protestant comparison (optional)

Catholic theology has parallels (sanctifying grace, beatific vision)
but the Catholic emphasis falls on the visio Dei rather than energetic
participation. Protestant theology generally reframes deification as
union with Christ in justification + sanctification, downplaying the
participation language. The Orthodox lens of this pack treats theosis
as the leading edge of soteriology and renders it visible at level 20;
other lenses in the broader app keep their own emphasis.
