# YHWH — the All-Holy Trinity, Pantokrator

The one God of historic Christian confession in the mode the Eastern
Orthodox tradition holds him: the **All-Holy Trinity** — Father, Son, and
Holy Spirit, one in essence, undivided — and the **Pantokrator**, ruler
of all, whose icon under the dome of every Orthodox church gazes back at
the worshipper with the eyes of judgment and mercy together. The deity
entry under this lens does not bracket the specifics that distinguish
Orthodoxy from her sisters; it leans in. Theosis is the throughline:
"God became man so that man might become god" (Athanasius, *On the
Incarnation* §54), participation in the **uncreated energies** of God
without ever exhausting his transcendent essence (Palamas). The Tabor
light of the Transfiguration is not metaphor but visible-uncreated
glory, and the saint at the peak of contemplation actually shines.

## Stat block

- **Edicts**: pursue union with God (theosis), keep the fasts, venerate
  the saints and icons, give alms
- **Anathema**: blaspheme the icons, despair of theosis, schism from the
  Body, presume upon grace
- **Areas of Concern**: theosis, liturgy, icons, ascesis, eucharist
- **Divine Attribute**: Wisdom or Charisma
- **Divine Font**: heal
- **Sanctification**: can choose holy
- **Divine Skill**: Religion
- **Favored Weapon**: longsword (the "sword of the Spirit" — Eph 6:17,
  Heb 4:12)
- **Domains**: Healing, Truth, Soul, Vigil
- **Alternate Domains**: Creation, Knowledge, Zeal
- **Cleric Spells**: 1st *bless*; 3rd *holy light*; 5th *divine wrath*
- **Religious Symbol**: Christ Pantokrator icon
- **Sacred Animals**: lamb (paschal) and eagle (John the Theologian)
- **Sacred Colors**: gold and red (royal porphyry)

## Icons are not idols

The single most important interpretive note for this lens. Following
**John of Damascus** and the **Seventh Ecumenical Council (Nicaea II,
787)**, the veneration (*proskynesis*) shown to icons is sharply
distinguished from the worship (*latreia*) due to God alone. The honor
given to the image passes to the prototype. Icons function in this pack
as **windows to heaven**, not as objects of magical compulsion — they
are anchors of memory, presence, and intercession, not levers. A
[[orthodox:class-thaumaturge]] who treats an icon as a charged battery
has misread the theology and likely the implement; the implement is
*given*, not *taken*.

## Theosis as the throughline

Soteriology in this pack is not justification-by-imputation alone but
**progressive deification**: katharsis (purification) → theoria
(illumination) → theosis (union). Levels 1–20 of a Christian-themed PC
arc map naturally onto this ladder, and a level-20 capstone feat-
equivalent grants the *Light* cantrip at-will plus a cosmetic glow
visible to spiritual sight ([[orthodox:reference-theosis]]). The
discipline that reaches it is hesychasm; its prayer is the Jesus Prayer
([[orthodox:class-monk]]).
