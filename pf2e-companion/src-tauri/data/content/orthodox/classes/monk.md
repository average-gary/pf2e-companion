# Monk — Iconographer / Hesychast (Orthodox-distinctive)

The headline class of the Orthodox pack. The PF2e Monk's Focus-spell
chassis ports almost too cleanly onto the **Athonite hesychast**
tradition — the contemplative monk who, through sustained practice of
the **Jesus Prayer**, descends his intellect into the heart and
participates in the **uncreated energies** of God. At the peak of
contemplation the saint **glows** — visibly, perceptibly, with the
same light that shone on Mount Tabor at the Transfiguration. This is
not metaphor; it is the Palamite claim. (See [[orthodox:gregory-palamas]]
on the essence/energies distinction; [[orthodox:reference-theosis]] on
the doctrine.)

The wiki worked example for the Orthodox lens calls this archetype out
by name: "Iconographer subclass; Hesychast Jesus-Prayer as a continuous
Focus-spell mechanic; uncreated light at level 20."

## Two complementary subclass paths

### Path of the Iconographer

The monk who writes (Orthodox usage: an icon is *written*, not
*painted*) sacred images under the rule of fasting, prayer, and the
canon of iconography. Mechanically: pairs with [[orthodox:class-thaumaturge]]
multiclass for the Mirror implement, or solo as a crafting-focused Monk
whose Ki / Focus pool empowers consecrated panels rather than martial
strikes. **Patron**: [[orthodox:john-of-damascus]] (the doctrinal
defender of the practice); secondary [[orthodox:jegudiel]] (the angel
who sanctifies labor).

### Path of the Hesychast

The monk of the inner prayer — the Athonite tradition. *Hesychia* =
"stillness." The monk descends his intellect from the head into the
heart and there practices the Jesus Prayer continuously. Mechanically:
the **Jesus Prayer Focus-spell mechanic** below. **Patron**:
[[orthodox:gregory-palamas]] (doctor of hesychasm); secondary
[[orthodox:selaphiel]] (the angel of prayer).

## The Jesus Prayer as a continuous Focus-spell mechanic

> "Lord Jesus Christ, Son of God, have mercy on me, a sinner."

The Jesus Prayer is, in real Orthodox practice, prayed continuously,
joined to the breath, until it descends from the lips into the mind
and from the mind into the heart. *The Way of a Pilgrim* describes the
practice's stages.

**Mechanic** (PF2e Focus-spell port):

- The Hesychast Monk gains a **dedicated Focus pool** of up to 3 points,
  refreshed by the *Refocus* activity — flavored as ten minutes of the
  Jesus Prayer, joined to the breath. (No real-world prayer is required
  — see "DragonRaid trap" below.)
- A **continuous-state Focus spell** ("the Inner Prayer") may be
  *Sustained* for free at the start of each combat round provided the
  monk has not begun and is not in a *clamor* condition (heavy social
  effects, deafened, etc.). While Sustained: **+1 status to Will saves
  vs mental and emotion effects**, and demonic / unholy creatures
  approaching within 30 ft become aware they are being observed.
- A **toll-house defense activation** (level 7+ feat): when present at
  any of the [[orthodox:cosmology-aerial-toll-houses]] stations, the
  Inner Prayer's bonus extends to the entire party and reduces by 1
  the DC of the station's "produce a receipt" trial. This is the
  rules-side surface of the Orthodox tradition that the toll houses
  can be **passed through unmolested** by the soul that has lived in
  the prayer.

### **The DragonRaid trap (mandatory)**

The cast trigger is a **spell action**. The player **does not** need to
recite the Jesus Prayer aloud, fast, attend liturgy, or perform any
real-world piety. The character is praying; the player is rolling
dice. A player whose religious commitments differ from the character's
must be able to play the Hesychast Monk without acting the practice
out. **Real-world prayer is not a mechanical input.** This is a
non-negotiable design constraint of the entire pack and is most
load-bearing here.

## Uncreated light at level 20 (the capstone)

The Hesychast Monk's level-20 capstone — and the parallel
[[orthodox:reference-theosis]] capstone available to any class — is the
**Tabor-light glow**. The character gains the *Light* cantrip at-will
and a cosmetic glow visible to creatures with spiritual sight (any
creature with the *Detect Magic* / *Read Aura* / *True Seeing* surface,
or any celestial / fiend by default). The glow is **not a damage
trait**, **not a buff**, and **not a save bonus** — it is **fitness
for the new creation** ([[orthodox:cosmology-new-jerusalem]]) made
visible early. Mechanically cosmetic; narratively the entire arc.

The hesychast saints — Symeon the New Theologian, Gregory of Sinai,
Seraphim of Sarov (whose conversation with Motovilov in the snow is the
locus classicus) — exhibited this light in their lifetimes. Build the
PC arc toward it.

## Build recipe

1. **Class**: Monk (Path of the Hesychast or Path of the Iconographer).
2. **Deity**: [[orthodox:yhwh]] — though the Monk's relationship is
   mediated through patron archangel ([[orthodox:selaphiel]]) and
   doctor-saint ([[orthodox:gregory-palamas]] /
   [[orthodox:john-of-damascus]]) more than the Cleric's is.
3. **Focus pool**: Inner Prayer (Path of Hesychast) or Iconographic
   Consecration (Path of Iconographer).
4. **Multiclass natural fits**: [[orthodox:class-cleric]] cloistered
   (hieromonk); [[orthodox:class-thaumaturge]] (icon Mirror).

## Honest gotchas

- **Hesychasm is a real living tradition.** Mount Athos is on the map.
  The Philokalia is in print. Treat it with care; this is not D&D
  Eastern-mysticism flavoring.
- **The level-20 glow must remain cosmetic.** If you mechanize it
  (damage, save bonus, healing pulse), you have made the player's
  character's sanctity a power-budget calculation. Don't.
- **The Iconographer path is craft-flavored.** It does not turn the
  Monk into a caster; the Focus pool empowers consecrated objects in
  downtime, not in initiative.
