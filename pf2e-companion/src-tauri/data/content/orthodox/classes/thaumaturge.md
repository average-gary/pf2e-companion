# Thaumaturge — pilgrim of icons and relics

The Orthodox Thaumaturge is the **most natural fit** of any class in
this pack. The Greek *thaumaturgos* — "wonderworker" — is itself a
title of Orthodox saints (Nicholas the Wonderworker, Spyridon the
Wonderworker, Gregory the Wonderworker of Neocaesarea). The class's
nine implements map cleanly onto Orthodox sacred objects, with **icons
on the Mirror implement** as the Orthodox-distinctive headline.

## The Mirror / icon implement — the headline

The Orthodox Thaumaturge whose primary implement is the **Mirror**
carries an **icon panel** — most often a small portable encolpion-icon
of Christ Pantokrator or the Theotokos Hodegetria. The icon is a
**window**, per [[orthodox:john-of-damascus]]: the Thaumaturge's effects
are not the icon emitting power but the prototype acting through the
window when veneration is offered. **The honor passes to the
prototype.**

This frames every Mirror-implement effect as **petition + assent**, not
as **command**. The class fantasy is exactly right: a saint who carries
a sacred object, drawing on its consecrated power without claiming it
as her own.

## Honest gotchas

- **Icons are not idols.** This is the load-bearing distinction of the
  Orthodox lens. The Thaumaturge who treats her icon as a magic
  battery has misread the theology. Treat the implement as *given* —
  blessed by a hierarch, inherited from a predecessor monastic, found
  on pilgrimage to a wonder-working shrine.
- **No *pharmakeia* problem.** Effects are sign-bearing, not
  drug-mediated; the implement is a token of faith, not a technique.

## Saint / wonderworker patrons

- **Patron Nicholas the Wonderworker** — sea travel, justice for the
  unjustly accused, the secret giving of alms.
- **Patron Spyridon** — answer to slander, charisms of teaching.
- **Patron Gregory the Wonderworker** — the Neocaesarean tradition of
  driving demons out by the sign of the cross.
