# Sorcerer — the Angelic bloodline canonical

The Sorcerer is the magic-saturated class with the cleanest reskin
**only because the Remaster shipped an Angelic bloodline as core**:
Divine tradition with explicit "descended from celestials" flavor. The
Orthodox lens has an additional advantage — the **Ethiopian Orthodox
canonicity of 1 Enoch** lets the Watcher mythology be cited as
in-canon for that strand of the tradition without the Protestant-style
hedge.

## Bloodline verdict table

| Bloodline | Verdict | Reskin |
|---|---|---|
| **Angelic** | **Ship — canonical** | Descended from a faithful Watcher; or from a saint's lineage; or from a unique calling like Jeremiah's "before I formed you in the womb I knew you." |
| Aberrant | Reframe → tragic-arc | Watcher-corrupted lineage; play as redemption arc. |
| Imperial | Ship | Davidic / Byzantine imperial line; messianic ancestry as power. |
| Wyrmblood | Ship (cautious) | Leviathan / Behemoth descent from Job 40-41. |
| Genie | Ship | Faithful elemental Watchers (the obedient remainder per 1 Enoch 6). |
| Psychopomp | Ship | Death-attendant angels of Sheol and the Boneyard ([[orthodox:cosmology-sheol]]). |
| **Demonic** | Cut or villain-only | Descent from a fallen Watcher (Azazel-line). PC tragic-arc only. |
| **Diabolic** | Cut or villain-only | Descent from the Lucifer-fall hierarchy. Same. |
| **Hag** | Cut | Mediumistic / Endor-line. Deut 18 forbidden territory. |
| **Shadow** | Cut | Sheol-haunted; better as NPC. |
| **Undead** | Cut | Hard cut — `void` trait is anathema. |

## Magic-theology framing (Charism + Lewisian hybrid)

The Angelic sorcerer draws on the obedient-Watcher remainder; the
redeemed Aberrant sorcerer wrestles with a corrupted lineage; the
Demonic / Diabolic NPC sorcerer is the antagonist. Tolkien's Letter
155 ("the supremely bad motive is domination of other 'free' wills")
is the line.

## Honest gotchas

- **Don't moralize at the table.** A Christian-themed game in which
  the Sorcerer is constantly being lectured isn't fun.
- **The Demonic / Diabolic bloodlines as tragic-arc PCs** require GM
  buy-in. If your table isn't ready for that weight, just cut them.
- **Ethiopian Orthodox 1 Enoch canonicity** is a real flavor lever —
  a Sorcerer whose ancestry traces to a named Watcher (faithful or
  fallen) has scriptural footing rather than hedged tradition.
