# Oracle — fool-for-Christ and prophet

The PF2e Remaster Oracle's **cursebound** mechanic — power that comes
with progressively worsening physical or social cost — fits two
Orthodox-distinctive vocations almost perfectly: the **Old Testament
prophet** (per the Lewisian-pack pattern) and the **fool-for-Christ**
(*yurodivy* / *salos*) — the saint who takes on apparent madness for
the sake of confounding worldly wisdom. St Basil the Blessed of Moscow
walked naked in winter and rebuked Ivan the Terrible to his face;
St Xenia of St Petersburg wandered the city in her dead husband's
uniform.

## Build recipe

1. **Mystery** (reflavor as "calling"):
   - **Lore** mystery → scribal prophet (Daniel, Ezra) or learned
     hesychast.
   - **Battle** mystery → herald of judgment (Elijah on Carmel; the
     fool-for-Christ rebuking the prince).
   - **Cosmos** mystery → apocalyptic visionary (Daniel; 2 Esdras 13;
     Athonite vision tradition).
   - **Tempest** mystery → storm-and-fire prophet (Elijah).
   - **Life** mystery → resurrection-and-healing prophet (Elijah of
     widow's son; the wonderworker tradition — Sts Nicholas the
     Wonderworker, Spyridon).
2. **Curse**: render as **the prophetic burden** (Jeremiah's burning
   bones) **or the foolishness of the fool-for-Christ** (apparent
   madness, social rejection, the body's signs of grace seen as
   shame).
3. **Deity**: [[orthodox:yhwh]] — the Mystery is your calling, not a
   deity slot.

## Honest gotchas

- **No rules change needed.** Cursebound translates one-for-one.
- **Fool-for-Christ is sensitive material.** Don't reduce real
  mental illness to a class flavor; the *yurodivye* are saints whose
  apparent madness is a chosen ascesis, theologically distinct. GM
  buy-in matters.
- **The prophetic burden is opt-in, not punishment.** Players should
  embrace the cursebound trade-off knowingly.
