# Champion — the Orthodox warrior saint

The Remaster Champion is a clean reskin under the Orthodox lens. The
**roster of warrior-saints** does the heavy lifting Catholic Champions
get from St George and St Michael; the Orthodox version adds **Sts
Demetrios of Thessaloniki, Theodore Stratelates, Theodore Tyron, and
Mercurius**, plus the Synaxis of Seven Archangels for direct angelic
patronage.

## Build recipe

1. **Deity**: [[orthodox:yhwh]].
2. **Sanctification**: holy.
3. **Cause**:
   - **Justice** (paladin) — warrior-saint pattern (Demetrios,
     Theodore Stratelates, Mercurius), or [[orthodox:michael]] direct
     patron.
   - **Liberation** (liberator) — the harrowing-of-Hades pattern;
     [[orthodox:michael]] in the toll houses
     ([[orthodox:cosmology-aerial-toll-houses]]).
   - **Redemption** (redeemer) — [[orthodox:raphael]] /
     [[orthodox:barachiel]]; the household-protective valence.
4. **Edicts/anathema**: lift from [[orthodox:yhwh]] plus archangel or
   warrior-saint overlay.

## Honest gotchas

- **Champion's Aura** is mechanically clean but flavor-loaded.
  Recommend "the Glory" / *doxa* — the Tabor-light radiance the
  Champion carries into battle. This is **not the same** as the
  level-20 theosis glow ([[orthodox:reference-theosis]]); the aura is
  a vocational gift, the glow is the eschatological fitness.
- **Holy / Unholy damage** is now a real trait. Orthodox demons
  (Watcher-line, Lucifer-line, toll-house prosecutors) take holy.
- **Icons-as-implements** belong on the [[orthodox:class-thaumaturge]]
  side; the Champion's relationship to icons is veneration, not
  instrumental.

## Saint / archangel patron variants

- **Patron Michael** — Justice or Liberation; Archistrategos overlay.
- **Patron Demetrios** — Justice; city-defender flavor.
- **Patron George** — Justice (dragon-slayer; shared with the
  Catholic lens but also fully native to Orthodox iconography).
- **Patron Raphael** — Redemption; household-healer overlay.
