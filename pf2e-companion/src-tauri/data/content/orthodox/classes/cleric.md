# Cleric — priest, deacon, or hieromonk

The Orthodox Cleric is a **priest, deacon, or hieromonk** (priest-monk).
The lens is **continuationist**: charisms remain active, the gifts of
1 Cor 12 are gameable, and the Cleric's spell list is read through that
frame — every casting is a charism, not a technique. The Septuagint
canon plus 1-3 Maccabees, Psalm 151, and the Prayer of Manasseh widens
the prayer-and-doctrine corpus the in-fiction Cleric draws from.

## Build recipe

1. **Deity**: [[orthodox:yhwh]].
2. **Doctrine**:
   - **Cloistered cleric** = monastic / hieromonk / scribal — pairs
     with [[orthodox:class-monk]] for a hesychast Cleric/Monk
     multiclass.
   - **Warpriest** = parish priest who walks with a Champion-led order,
     or a chaplain of warrior-saint cults (Demetrios, Theodore).
3. **Divine Font**: **heal** for canonical clergy. *Harm* is reserved
   for explicitly fallen / apostate clergy.
4. **Anathema**: *void-trait* spells are anathema for canonical clergy.

## Theosis as the throughline

The Orthodox Cleric's spell progression maps onto the threefold ladder:
**katharsis → theoria → theosis** ([[orthodox:reference-theosis]]).
Levels 1-7 are the work of purification (the Cleric's discipline of
fast and confession is in-fiction; the mechanics are unchanged); 8-14
are the work of illumination (visions, augury, true seeing); 15-20 are
the work of union, culminating in the level-20 capstone glow. The
Cleric is the class for which this arc is most naturally readable at
the table.

## Charism not technique (the DragonRaid trap)

**Do not require the player to recite prayers, fast for real, or perform
real-world piety to cast spells.** The Jesus Prayer mechanic on
[[orthodox:class-monk]] is a **spell action**, not a real-world utterance.
The Cleric's spell list is *flavored* by the charism frame; it is not
gated by it. A player whose religious commitments differ from the
character's must be able to play the character without acting them.

## Domain choices

Primary: Healing, Truth, Soul, Vigil. Alternate: Creation, Knowledge,
Zeal. ([[orthodox:yhwh]] base; substitute or narrow for archangel /
saint patrons — Raphael→Healing primary, Michael→Zeal+Vigil primary,
Selaphiel→Soul+Vigil primary.)
