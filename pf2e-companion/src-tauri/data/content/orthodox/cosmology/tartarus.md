# Tartarus — the prison of the fallen Watchers

The deepest pit, where rebellious angels are "kept in chains under
darkness for the judgment of the great day" (2 Peter 2:4; Jude 6). The
1 Enoch tradition — **canonical for Ethiopian Orthodox**, received as
pious tradition for the rest of the Orthodox world — names the bound:
Azazel under jagged rocks in Dudâêl, Semjâzâ and his hosts under the
hills for seventy generations. **Uriel is set over Tartarus** (1 Enoch
20:2); see [[orthodox:uriel]].

## PF2e plane mapping

Maps to the **Tartarus floor of the Abyss** (or to a sealed sub-layer
of Hell, depending on campaign geography). **Distinct** from
[[orthodox:cosmology-gehenna]] (the eschatological lake of fire) and
the wider [[orthodox:cosmology-abyss]] / *tehom*.

## Campaign hooks

- A high-level dungeon descent to recover a pre-Flood relic — a
  fragment of forbidden craft per 1 Enoch 8 — is a classic Tartarus
  expedition.
- The Watcher catalogue is theologically loaded loot. PCs who recover
  it must decide whether to destroy, study, or wield.
