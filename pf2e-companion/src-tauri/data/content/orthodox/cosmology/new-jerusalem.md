# the New Jerusalem — the eschaton

The city descending out of heaven from God; the new heavens and new
earth in which righteousness dwells. Heaven and earth are not destroyed
and replaced; they are renewed and joined, and the dwelling of God is
with mortals. The lake of fire receives Death and Hades themselves
([[orthodox:cosmology-gehenna]]).

## Orthodox-specific framing

Theosis comes to its eschatological completion here: the cosmos itself
becomes capable of receiving the uncreated light without veil. Maximus
the Confessor's *Ambigua* 7 — creation as becoming-divine through the
incarnate Logos — is the Orthodox theological frame for this scene.

## PF2e plane mapping

There is no vanilla PF2e plane that maps cleanly. **The New Jerusalem
is a campaign endgame state** — the post-eschaton fusion of the
Material Plane with [[orthodox:cosmology-heaven]]. A 20+ level
adventure path that ends with the descent of the city or with the
players present at the marriage supper of the Lamb is the natural
shape of an Orthodox campaign's capstone, with the level-20
[[orthodox:reference-theosis]] glow as the PCs' visible fitness for
the new creation.

## Mechanical notes

- *Heal* is at-will inside the city; *Resurrect* is unnecessary.
- The river of life and tree of life are encounter-tier loot for the
  capstone scene, granted as narrative resolution, not commodity.
- **There is no temple** (Rev 21:22) — the Lord God Almighty and the
  Lamb are its temple.
