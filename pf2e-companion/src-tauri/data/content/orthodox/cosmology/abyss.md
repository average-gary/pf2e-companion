# the Abyss / *tehom* — the deep

The chaos-deep over which the Spirit hovered in Genesis 1:2, present
before the ordering of creation. In Revelation it is opened by a key,
it looses locusts, it swallows the dragon for a thousand years.
Distinct from final Hell ([[orthodox:cosmology-gehenna]]) and from the
specific Watcher-pit of [[orthodox:cosmology-tartarus]]; the Abyss is
**the wider primordial deep**, both prison and unmade-place.

## PF2e plane mapping

Maps to the **Abyss** in vanilla PF2e nomenclature. In the Orthodox
double-origin tradition for fiends:

- **Devils** = Lucifer-fall hierarchy → courts of
  [[orthodox:cosmology-gehenna]].
- **Demons** = Watcher-fall hierarchy → ride out from the Abyss; the
  named ringleaders are bound in [[orthodox:cosmology-tartarus]];
  lesser shades roam the wider deep.
- **Daemons** = Revelation horsemen / personifications of plague,
  famine, death → lurk along the Abyss's edges.
- **Toll-house demons** ([[orthodox:cosmology-aerial-toll-houses]])
  rise from the Abyss to the aerial realm to prosecute souls in
  transit.

## Campaign hooks

- The dragon's binding for a thousand years (Revelation 20) is
  campaign-scale: it ends.
- Maritime / cthonic-deep dungeon flavor distinct from Gehenna's
  furnace; Job's Leviathan and Ezekiel's Pharaoh-as-sea-monster are
  the source register.
