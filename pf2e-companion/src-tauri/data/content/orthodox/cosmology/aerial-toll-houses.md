# the Aerial Toll Houses — post-mortem mega-dungeon

**The headline distinctive of the Orthodox lens.** A theologoumenon
(pious tradition, not dogma — disputed within Orthodoxy itself; David
Bentley Hart calls it heretical, Seraphim Rose defends it; not all
Orthodox accept it) describing what happens between death and judgment.
The departing soul is escorted by angels through the **aerial realm**
of Ephesians 6 — the lower heavens of the *exousia tou aeros* — and at
each of (typically) twenty toll-stations, demons accuse the soul of a
specific sin category. Each station demands evidence of compensatory
good deeds or repentance; angelic witnesses speak for the soul; the
soul produces (or fails to produce) the receipts.

The locus classicus is **Theodora's vision** in the 10th-century *Life
of Basil the Younger*. The number twenty is conventional, not fixed.

The wiki's worked example for the Orthodox lens names this entry
explicitly: **"Aerial Toll Houses as a literal level-15+ post-mortem
mega-dungeon"**
([[wiki:pf2e-biblical-reskin/denominational-lens-decision § Eastern
Orthodox worked example]]). What follows is the campaign-grade scaffold.

## The twenty toll-stations

Each station is a thematic encounter zone with a sin, a demon-prosecutor
(Power-tier or Principality-tier per [[orthodox:cosmology-heaven]]), and
an angelic defender from the Synaxis of Seven. Run as a sequential
"plane crawl" between death and Hades / heaven; PCs may enter alive on a
rescue mission for a recently-deceased ally, or arrive *in articulo
mortis* for a Pelagian campaign capstone.

## Encounter shape (level 15-to-20)

- **Level 15-16 (early stations 1-7)**: idle words → avarice. Social /
  trial-of-the-soul encounters with prosecutorial demons. Skill-heavy;
  *Diplomacy*, *Deception* (used wickedly), *Religion* checks against
  the demons' accusations. PC must produce *receipts* — narrative
  callbacks to repented sins, alms given, fasts kept, prayers prayed.
  GM tracks this through the campaign in advance; this is **not** a
  surprise mechanic. The Jesus Prayer Focus-spell from
  [[orthodox:class-monk]] is fully load-bearing here.

- **Level 17-18 (mid stations 8-14)**: usury → murder. Combat
  encounters with Power-tier demons. Holy / Unholy damage traits
  matter; PC's sanctification ([[orthodox:yhwh]]) and the angelic
  defender's aid are the difference between being dragged to
  [[orthodox:cosmology-gehenna]] or pressing on.

- **Level 19-20 (late stations 15-20)**: sorcery → unmercifulness.
  Principality-tier prosecutors. The Pride station (11, run late as a
  capstone) confronts the PCs with the Lucifer-pattern accuser
  himself; defeating him through humility (an **opt-out** mechanic,
  not a damage roll) is the throughline. The Heresy station
  showcases [[orthodox:john-of-damascus]] in person; the
  Unmercifulness station is the final test before the Pantokrator's
  judgment.

## Theological status flags (mandatory)

- **Theologoumenon, not dogma.** Tables that prefer the Catholic
  abstract-purgatory model, or that follow Hart in rejecting toll
  houses, may omit this entry without breaking the rest of the pack.
- **Number twenty is conventional, not fixed.** GMs should feel free
  to compress to a 7- or 12-station version for a shorter dungeon.
- **The receipts are a narrative tracking device, not a piety meter.**
  The DragonRaid trap is forbidden: the player does not need to recite
  real prayers or perform real-world acts. The PC's repented sins and
  remembered alms are tracked in fiction.

## Campaign hooks

- **The Theodora rescue**: a recently-departed NPC ally is in transit;
  PCs descend (or rather, ascend the aerial realm) to deliver
  intercession.
- **The reverse-toll-house**: a demon-prosecutor flees the realm to
  evade reform after a saint's intercession; PCs hunt it.
- **The standing-stone toll**: a station where a famous historical
  saint left a permanent witness — narrative loot.
