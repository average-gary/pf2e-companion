# Sheol / Hades — the abode of the dead (intermediate state)

The morally-neutral Old Testament abode of the dead, rendered Hades by
the Septuagint and the New Testament. In Luke 16 it has compartments —
"Abraham's bosom" and a place of torment, separated by a great chasm.
For the Orthodox lens, Hades is **emptied at Pascha**: the Anastasis
icon shows the risen Christ trampling the broken gates and pulling
Adam and Eve from their tombs. This is the iconographic signature of
the harrowing of Hades.

## PF2e plane mapping

Maps to the **Boneyard** ferry zone — the pre-judgment intermediate
state where souls await sorting. **Distinct** from
[[orthodox:cosmology-gehenna]] (the lake of fire),
[[orthodox:cosmology-tartarus]] (the Watcher prison), and the
[[orthodox:cosmology-aerial-toll-houses]] (the post-mortem demonic
prosecution that *takes place between death and Hades* in the toll-house
tradition). Pharasma's vanilla gatekeeping function reskins as
delegated authority under YHWH, not autonomous deity.

## Campaign hooks

- The harrowing of Hades is the canonical pattern for any descent: a
  Cleric or Champion who treads it walks the road of Christ between
  Friday and Sunday.
- *Raise Dead* and *Resurrect* reach into Sheol (or the toll houses if
  the soul is *en route*); each casting carries the moral weight of
  recalling a soul from intermediate rest.
