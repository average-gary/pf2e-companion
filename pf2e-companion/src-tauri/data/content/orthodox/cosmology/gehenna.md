# Gehenna — the lake of fire

The Valley of Hinnom outside Jerusalem; the rubbish-and-corpse fire
that became the New Testament's name for the eschatological place of
punishment. The lake of fire of Revelation 20–21. Final-state, not
intermediate; reserved for the unrepentant and for the dragon, the
beast, the false prophet, Death and Hades themselves.

## Orthodox interpretive note

A strand of Eastern Orthodox theology (Isaac the Syrian, parts of the
Cappadocian tradition) holds that the **fire is God's love**
experienced as torment by those whose hearts have refused him; the
saved and the damned both stand in the same divine presence and
respond differently. This is a homiletic tradition, not dogma; the
Orthodox lens preserves the **lake-of-fire imagery** at face value
while permitting tables to render the underlying experience either as
extrinsic punishment or as the river of fire from the divine throne
turned bitter to those who hate it.

## PF2e plane mapping

Maps to **Hell** (the lawful evil layer in vanilla nomenclature).
Pride-fall fiends (the Lucifer-line devils) populate Gehenna's courts
and prisons.

## Mechanical notes

- Sanctification of inhabitants: nearly all unholy.
- *Banishment* sends infernal creatures here as their original plane.
- The toll-house demons ([[orthodox:cosmology-aerial-toll-houses]])
  who succeed in their prosecution drag the soul *here*; this is the
  load-bearing campaign-stake for the toll-house dungeon.
