# Heaven — the Throne Room beneath the Pantokrator

Where the throne is. The throne room of Revelation 4–5; the third heaven
to which Paul was caught up; the sea of glass; the four living
creatures and the twenty-four elders; and crowning the dome, the
**Christ Pantokrator** — ruler of all — gazing down upon the courts.
The Hagia Sophia translates the place into stone and gold; every
Orthodox church reproduces the schema.

## PF2e plane mapping

Maps to the **Empyrean** / Heaven plane. The full Pseudo-Dionysian
nine-rank hierarchy is in force here, with the **seven-archangel
Synaxis** at its forefront — Michael, Gabriel, Raphael, Uriel,
Selaphiel, Jegudiel, Barachiel. Reskin Paizo's celestial creature
ladder onto the Pseudo-Dionysian scheme: Solar / Empyrean for Seraphim
and Cherubim; Planetar / Astral Deva for the governance triad
(Dominions / Virtues / Powers); the engaged triad
(Principalities / Archangels / Angels) for the named patrons and the
guardian of every baptized soul.

## Orthodox-specific notes

- **Icons of the throne**: the Pantokrator dome and the Hodegetria
  Theotokos panel are gameable as scrying surfaces / buff-anchors at
  high tiers; see [[orthodox:class-thaumaturge]] for implement framing.
- **Necromancy and consult-the-dead spells do not function here** — the
  saints in glory are not on call. Intercession is asked of them
  (Tobit 12:15, Rev 8:3-4 via [[orthodox:selaphiel]]); it is not
  compelled.
