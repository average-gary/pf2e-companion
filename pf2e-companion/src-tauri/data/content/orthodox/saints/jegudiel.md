# Holy Archangel Jegudiel, the Glorifier

The angel who rewards labor and teaches glorification — the work of God
in the worker. Iconography depicts Jegudiel with a golden crown in the
right hand (for those who labor for the kingdom) and a triple-thonged
scourge in the left (for those who reject the labor). His office is the
sanctification of toil: every honest craft, every monastic obedience,
every pilgrim mile is offered through him to the Father. Venerated, not
worshipped.

## Cleric / Champion patron

Cleric takes the **Zeal** or **Vigil** domain. Champion cause **Justice**
or **Liberation** with a labor-and-craft overlay (the patron of the
guild as much as the army).

- **Edicts (additional)**: labor as unto the Lord (Col 3:23), reward
  honest craft, finish what you have begun.
- **Anathema (additional)**: take wages without work, despise the
  worker, abandon a vow undertaken in haste.

## Mechanical hooks

- Crafting downtime in Jegudiel's aspect grants narrative-tier bonuses
  to consumable creation (gold-leaf, censers, icon panels).
- *Heroism* and *Zealous Conviction* fit Jegudiel's reward-of-labor
  patronage.
