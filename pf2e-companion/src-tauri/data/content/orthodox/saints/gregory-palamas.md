# Saint Gregory Palamas, Doctor of Hesychasm

The 14th-century Athonite monk and Archbishop of Thessaloniki whose
defense of the hesychast monks against Barlaam of Calabria produced the
**essence/energies distinction**: humans participate in God's uncreated
*energies* (operations, grace, light) but never in his transcendent
*essence*. This is the hinge that makes **theosis** coherent — creatures
genuinely become "partakers of the divine nature" (2 Pet 1:4) without
becoming God. The Tabor light of the Transfiguration is the visible
sign: physical, perceptible, but uncreated.

## Cleric / Monk patron

Cleric takes the **Soul** or **Truth** domain. The Iconographer/Hesychast
**Monk** ([[orthodox:class-monk]]) takes Palamas as the doctor of his
discipline; the level-20 capstone glow ([[orthodox:reference-theosis]])
is rendered theologically by Palamas's framework.

- **Edicts (additional)**: practice the inner prayer, defend the
  uncreated light against rationalist reduction, descend the intellect
  into the heart.
- **Anathema (additional)**: collapse the essence/energies distinction
  into pantheism, treat hesychasm as technique rather than gift,
  despise the simple monk for the learned theologian.

## Mechanical hooks

- *Light* cantrip rendered as a manifestation of uncreated energy —
  cosmetic in early levels, mechanical at the level-20 capstone.
- *True Strike* and *Foresight* as Tabor-light visions of the contemplative.
