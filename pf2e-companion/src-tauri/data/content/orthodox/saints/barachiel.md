# Holy Archangel Barachiel, the Blessing of God

The angel who distributes blessings — the patron of family life,
hospitality, the household economy, and the abundance of the table.
Iconography shows Barachiel scattering white rose petals from a fold of
his red mantle: the petals are the discrete graces given for marriages,
births, harvests, and the small mercies of ordinary life. Venerated,
not worshipped (see [[orthodox:john-of-damascus]]).

## Cleric / Champion patron

Cleric takes the **Family** or **Healing** domain. Champion cause
**Redemption** (redeemer) — the household-protective valence is
strong; Barachiel pairs naturally with [[orthodox:raphael]] for a
home-and-pilgrimage cleric.

- **Edicts (additional)**: bless the table and the threshold, welcome
  the stranger as Christ (Mt 25:35), keep the marriage covenant.
- **Anathema (additional)**: defraud the household, refuse hospitality
  to the road-weary, curse a child.

## Mechanical hooks

- *Bless* and *Heal* as Barachiel's signature.
- *Hero's Defiance* in defense of family or household members triggers
  flavor-only auto-stabilize.
