# Holy Archangel Raphael, the Healer

The angel of Tobit — companion on the road, healer of Tobit's blindness
and binder of Asmodeus, who in 12:15 reveals himself as "one of the
seven holy angels who present the prayers of the saints and enter into
the presence of the Holy One." That verse is the **scriptural anchor
for the Orthodox count of seven archangels** (Tobit being canonical in
the Septuagint, which the Orthodox lens uses without footnote). His name
means "God heals."

## Cleric / Champion patron

Cleric of Raphael takes the **Healing** domain (primary). Champion
takes cause **Redemption** (redeemer) — Raphael's aid to Tobit and
Sarah maps cleanly to the redeemer's reactive intervention.

- **Edicts (additional)**: tend the sick without partiality, accompany
  pilgrims on dangerous roads, bind demons that prey on the household.
- **Anathema (additional)**: refuse aid to a stranger, profit from
  another's affliction.

## Mechanical hooks

- *Heal* divine font is core; *Cleanse Affliction* is signature.
- *Banishment* against household-spirit antagonists fits the
  Asmodeus-binding scene of Tobit 8.
