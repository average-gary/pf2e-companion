# Holy Archangel Selaphiel, the Intercessor

The angel of prayer. Selaphiel appears in Orthodox tradition rather than
in canonical Scripture by name, but his role is anchored in **Tobit
12:15** (the seven who present the saints' prayers) and **Revelation
8:3-4** (the angel with the golden censer who offers the prayers of all
the saints upon the heavenly altar). Iconography shows him with head
inclined and hands crossed in the orant posture — the angel who teaches
the body of prayer. Venerated, not worshipped (see
[[orthodox:john-of-damascus]]).

## Cleric / Monk patron

Cleric takes the **Soul** or **Vigil** domain. The
**Iconographer/Hesychast Monk** ([[orthodox:class-monk]]) finds in
Selaphiel her natural patron — the Jesus Prayer is Selaphiel's office.

- **Edicts (additional)**: keep watch in prayer, intercede for the
  living and the dead, censer the temple, teach others the inner
  prayer.
- **Anathema (additional)**: pray to be seen of men (Mt 6:5), break the
  rule of prayer out of sloth, despise the prayers of the simple.

## Mechanical hooks

- Continuous Focus-spell (the Jesus Prayer) refresh windows lengthen
  while in Selaphiel's aspect — see [[orthodox:class-monk]] for the
  mechanic.
- *Sanctuary* and *Bless* are Selaphiel's signature.
