# Holy Archangel Uriel, the Fire of God

"Uriel" — *the fire of God*. The angel who interrogates Ezra in 2 Esdras
and who in the 1 Enoch tradition warns Noah of the flood and is "set
over the world and over Tartarus" (1 Enoch 20:2). The Orthodox lens
counts Uriel among the seven archangels of the Synaxis without
qualification — no Protestant-style hedge needed. His icon shows him
holding a flaming sword or a tongue of fire; venerated, not worshipped.

## Cleric / Oracle patron

Cleric of Uriel takes the **Fire** or **Truth** domain. Oracle takes
the **Cosmos** or **Battle** mystery — warning-of-judgment and
flame-of-revelation make a strong cursebound fit
([[orthodox:class-oracle]]).

- **Edicts (additional)**: warn the unwary of coming judgment, illumine
  what is hidden, witness against falsehood.
- **Anathema (additional)**: hide a true vision, profit from the
  ignorance of another, allow a covenant to be desecrated unchecked.

## Mechanical hooks

- *Holy Light* and *Sunburst* as Uriel's fire.
- *Foresight* as the canonical Uriel-aspected vision spell.
