# Holy Archangel Gabriel, the Annunciator

The herald of the Synaxis. Gabriel interprets visions to Daniel,
announces John the Forerunner's conception to Zechariah, and bears the
Annunciation to the Theotokos — the moment Orthodox iconography most
loves to render, lily in hand, gold leaf behind. His icon is venerated;
the honor passes to the prototype (see [[orthodox:john-of-damascus]] on
icons-not-idols).

## Cleric / Oracle patron

Cleric of Gabriel chooses the **Knowledge** or **Truth** domain;
Oracle takes the **Cosmos** or **Lore** mystery (the cosmic Word
announcing the times).

- **Edicts (additional)**: deliver true news at any cost, interpret
  visions and dreams faithfully, refuse to be a herald of comfortable
  lies.
- **Anathema (additional)**: speak as one who knows when one does not,
  silence a prophet, falsify a message.

## Mechanical hooks

- Bard composition spells reskin as Gabriel's annunciation.
- *Sending* and *Augury* as flavor for high-tier Gabriel-aspected
  servants.
