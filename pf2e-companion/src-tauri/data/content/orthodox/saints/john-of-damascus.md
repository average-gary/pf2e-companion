# Saint John of Damascus, Defender of the Icons

The 8th-century theologian whose three *Treatises on the Divine Images*
defended the veneration of icons against the Byzantine iconoclast
emperors and supplied the language adopted at **Nicaea II (787)**, the
Seventh Ecumenical Council: *proskynesis* (veneration, honor) is given
to the saints and their images; *latreia* (worship) is given to God
alone. **The honor given to the image passes to the prototype.** This
distinction is what makes every icon-mechanical hook in the Orthodox
pack theologically clean — icons are windows, not idols.

## Cleric / Thaumaturge patron

Cleric takes the **Truth** or **Knowledge** domain. The
[[orthodox:class-thaumaturge]] who builds around the **Mirror**
implement (icon-as-window) takes John as patron almost mandatorily.

- **Edicts (additional)**: defend the icons against blasphemy, teach
  the distinction of veneration from worship, write theology in the
  service of the Body.
- **Anathema (additional)**: treat an icon as an idol, treat an icon as
  mere paint, profit from desecration.

## Mechanical hooks

- *Detect Magic* + *Read Aura* on icons returns "window" signature, not
  "magic item" signature — flavor-only, but it disambiguates the
  Thaumaturge implement at the table.
- John's *Exact Exposition of the Orthodox Faith* fits as a rare-tier
  Tome implement.
