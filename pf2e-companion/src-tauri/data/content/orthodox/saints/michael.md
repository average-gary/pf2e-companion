# Holy Archangel Michael, Archistrategos

The **Archistrategos** — chief commander of the heavenly host. Daniel's
"one of the chief princes" who stands for the people of God; Jude's
disputant with the Devil over the body of Moses; Revelation's general
who casts down the dragon. In Orthodox iconography Michael is the
warrior of the Synaxis (the council of seven archangels), shown in red
mantle and military cuirass, often trampling the dragon underfoot. His
icon is venerated, not worshipped — the honor passes to the prototype
(Nicaea II); see [[orthodox:john-of-damascus]] for the doctrinal frame.
