# YHWH — the Lord, Father of the Spirit-Outpouring

The God of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob; the Father of our Lord Jesus Christ;
the One who poured out his Spirit upon the disciples at Pentecost (Acts 2)
in fulfillment of Joel's prophecy ("your sons and daughters shall
prophesy, your old men shall dream dreams, your young men shall see
visions" — Joel 2:28). The Pentecostal lens reads the Pentecost morning
as **the inaugural pattern of the present age**: the Spirit is *now*
poured out on all flesh; the charisms of 1 Cor 12 are active; tongues,
prophecy, healing, deliverance, and miracle are the ordinary means by
which the Body of Christ encounters the world.

The campaign-shaping consequence is **active spiritual-warfare
cosmology** ([[pentecostal:cosmology-abyss]]): demons are not abstract;
principalities and powers wage real war over geography and souls; the
Cleric and Oracle are *anointed combatants* in that war. Frank Peretti's
*This Present Darkness* (Crossway, 1986) is the explicit cultural
touchstone for tone — though the player-facing material here is purely
PF2e-mechanical.

## Stat block

- **Edicts**: obey the Spirit's leading, lay hands on the sick,
  proclaim freedom to captives, pursue holiness
- **Anathema**: grieve the Spirit, deny the gifts, profane the gathered
  church, love money
- **Areas of Concern**: Spirit, healing, prophecy, deliverance, evangelism
- **Divine Attribute**: Wisdom or Charisma
- **Divine Font**: heal
- **Sanctification**: can choose holy
- **Divine Skill**: Religion
- **Favored Weapon**: longsword (the "sword of the Spirit" — Eph 6:17)
- **Domains**: Healing, Truth, Zeal, Vigil
- **Alternate Domains**: Knowledge, Family, Confidence
- **Cleric Spells**: 1st *heal*; 3rd *holy light*; 5th *banishment*
- **Religious Symbol**: a cross with a descending dove
- **Sacred Animal**: dove (the Spirit at Christ's baptism — Matt 3:16;
  Acts 2's "rushing wind" descent)
- **Sacred Colors**: red (Spirit fire — the tongues of flame on the
  disciples, Acts 2:3) and white

## Magic-theology lens

The Pentecostal lens commits cleanly to **approach #1 (Charism)** in
[[magic-theology-approaches]]: Divine-tradition spells are charisms in
the literal 1 Cor 12 sense — gift of the Spirit, not technique of the
caster. See [[pentecostal:reference-charismata]] for the per-gift
mapping. **No mechanical change to PF2e**: this is flavor discipline.

The Cleric's *heal* font is the gift of healing manifest. The Oracle's
cursebound-mystery dynamic is the prophet's burden (Jer 20:9, 1 Cor
14:32). The Champion's aura is the Spirit's anointing (1 Sam 16:13;
Acts 10:38: "anointed with the Holy Spirit and with power"). Arcane,
Occult, and Primal traditions are theologically loaded — see
[[pentecostal:class-sorcerer]] for the Angelic-bloodline-only verdict.

## The DragonRaid caveat

**Do not require players to pray or speak in tongues at the table** to
cast spells. That is the DragonRaid trap (cf. wiki:prior-christian-rpg-
lessons): mechanizing piety as input. The mechanical surface is identical
to a vanilla PF2e Divine caster; what changes is the in-fiction framing.
A Cleric *describes* her cast as a charism; the player rolls dice as
normal.

## Spiritual warfare as campaign frame

Encounters with demons are routine, not rare. Deliverance is a *campaign
beat*, not a side quest. *Banishment* (5th) is on the deity's spell list
deliberately — every YHWH-aligned Cleric should be able to evict a
territorial spirit before mid-tier. See
[[pentecostal:cosmology-abyss]] for the operational geography.
