# The nine charisms of 1 Corinthians 12 — PF2e mapping

This is the **Pentecostal pack's distinctive content**: a per-charism
mapping of the nine gifts of 1 Cor 12:8-10 onto PF2e Divine-tradition
spells and class features. Use this table as the in-fiction explanation
for *every* Divine cast a YHWH-aligned PC makes.

> 1 Cor 12:7-11 — "But the manifestation of the Spirit is given to every
> man to profit withal. For to one is given by the Spirit the word of
> wisdom; to another the word of knowledge by the same Spirit; to
> another faith by the same Spirit; to another the gifts of healing by
> the same Spirit; to another the working of miracles; to another
> prophecy; to another discerning of spirits; to another divers kinds of
> tongues; to another the interpretation of tongues."

## The nine charisms mapped

| 1 Cor 12 charism | Function | PF2e mapping |
|---|---|---|
| **Word of wisdom** | Spirit-given counsel for the present moment | *augury* (4-hour-out wisdom); *guidance*; Bardic *inspire competence*; Oracle Lore mystery feats |
| **Word of knowledge** | Spirit-revealed facts otherwise inaccessible | *detect magic*, *read aura*, *true seeing*, Oracle's *vision*; the Cleric's *bless* as Spirit-given strategic knowledge |
| **Faith** | Mountain-moving trust (1 Cor 13:2; Mk 11:23) | *bless*, Champion's *Lay on Hands*, devotion auras, the Champion's Aura itself |
| **Gifts of healings** (plural) | Restoration of body | *heal*, *cleanse affliction* (Remaster *remove disease*), *restoration*, *sound body*; James 5:14 anointing-with-oil ritual flavor |
| **Workings of miracles** | Direct divine intervention beyond healing | *miracle*, *holy light*, *fly*, *walk on water*-flavored *air walk*; Elijah's *fireball* / *flame strike* (Carmel) |
| **Prophecy** | Foretelling and forth-telling | *augury* (longer-range vision), *foresight*, *vision*; the Oracle class's primary fictional axis |
| **Discerning of spirits** | Perception of hidden spiritual agencies | *see invisibility*, *detect alignment*-as-sanctification-trait detection (holy/unholy), *true seeing*; Champion's Sense Holiness / Sense Unholiness feats |
| **Divers kinds of tongues** | Spirit-given languages, known and unknown | *tongues* (literal), *comprehend languages*; Acts 2:4 as inaugural pattern |
| **Interpretation of tongues** | Render the Spirit-given utterance into the local language | *comprehend languages*, *translate*, *sending*; the partner-gift to tongues |

## Theological distinctions from "magic"

These are not techniques. Five distinctives separate charisms from any
other PF2e tradition's framing:

1. **Source** — the Holy Spirit, not training, study, bargain, or
   bloodline-as-power-ownership.
2. **Earnability** — gift, not earned. Cannot be purchased (Acts 8 /
   Simon Magus is the boundary case).
3. **Bearer** — manifestation *of the Spirit*, not of the gifted person.
   The gift is *borrowed*. The Cleric's healing is the Spirit's, not
   her own.
4. **Purpose** — communal edification (1 Cor 12:7 "for the common
   good"). Not for personal advantage. Counterfeit charisms (Acts 16:16
   Philippi slave girl) are detectable by their privatized purpose.
5. **Mode** — petition and submission, not technique and command.
   "Lord, send rain" not "Rain, come."

## How to use this in play

**Players**: when your YHWH-aligned PC casts a Divine-tradition spell,
optionally name the charism in fiction. "I lay hands and the Lord heals
through me" (gift of healings). "I see — I see what's actually moving
in this room" (discerning of spirits). The mechanical surface does not
change.

**GMs**: when YHWH-aligned NPCs cast, do the same. Counterfeit
charisms — used by villain Sorcerers and demonically empowered NPCs —
are detectable mechanically by *detect alignment*-as-unholy-trait or
narratively by their privatized, transactional, or self-aggrandizing
character.

**The DragonRaid caveat (mandatory)**. **Do not require players to pray,
recite scripture, or speak in tongues at the table to cast spells.**
Charisms are content and flavor, not mechanical input. The DragonRaid
trap — making piety a dice modifier — broke that game and would break
this one. A Pentecostal player may *describe* her Cleric's casting in
charismatic terms; an unbelieving player may *describe* it however they
like. Both roll dice the same way.

## Cessation note

1 Cor 13:8-10 ("when that which is perfect is come, then that which is
in part shall be done away") locates the cessation of charisms at the
**eschaton**, not at the close of the apostolic age. This is the
Pentecostal continuationist reading and the load-bearing distinctive of
the lens. See [[pentecostal:cosmology-new-jerusalem]] — within the New
Jerusalem, *tongues* and *prophecy* are no longer needed; the present
age uses them as standard kit.
