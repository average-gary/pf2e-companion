# the Abyss — the operational HQ of the present-age war

**This is the load-bearing cosmological entry of the Pentecostal pack.**
The Abyss is where the active demonic forces of the present age operate
from — the principalities and powers of Eph 6:12, the territorial
spirits of Daniel 10, the locust-host of Rev 9. **The present war is
fought here**, not in [[pentecostal:cosmology-gehenna]] (which is the
*future* sentence) and not in [[pentecostal:cosmology-tartarus]] (which
holds the *bound*, originally-fallen powers).

## PF2e plane mapping

Maps to the **Abyss** in vanilla PF2e nomenclature. The Pentecostal
demonology distinguishes:

- **Territorial spirits** — principalities assigned to nations, regions,
  and cities (Dan 10's "prince of Persia," "prince of Greece"). They
  contest geography. *Banishment* evicts; sustained intercession holds
  ground. Daniel's twenty-one-day fast (Dan 10:13) is the model.
- **Strongholds** — fortified ideological / cultural patterns (2 Cor
  10:4-5, "casting down imaginations and every high thing"). These are
  encounter sites in setting design.
- **Common demons** — the foot-soldiers of the host. *Detect alignment*
  / discernment-of-spirits gift (1 Cor 12:10) reveals them; *holy light*
  and *banishment* deal with them.

Frank Peretti's *This Present Darkness* (Crossway, 1986) is the
**explicit cultural touchstone** for the operational tone — invisible
spirit-on-spirit combat overlaying ordinary small-town life, prayer as a
mechanical input on the battle, deliverance ministry as the literal
front line. The novel is reference flavor, not doctrine.

## Spiritual-warfare framing — the campaign frame

A Pentecostal-lens campaign is **spiritual warfare as the explicit
genre**. Encounters with demons are routine, not exceptional. *Heal*,
*holy light*, *banishment*, *tongues*, and the discernment charism are
the standard kit. PCs are presumed to be operating against a
demonological backdrop the *world* takes seriously — local clergy,
NPCs, and the GM all play it straight.

## Campaign hooks

- A "prince of the city" antagonist (Dan 10 model) as the season-long
  big bad behind the surface villains.
- An exorcism set-piece adapted from Acts 16:16-18 (Paul at Philippi).
- The Rev 9 locust-release as a campaign-shifting eschatological event.
