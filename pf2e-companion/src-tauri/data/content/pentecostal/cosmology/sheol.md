# Sheol / Hades — the abode of the dead

The intermediate state. The Pentecostal lens reads Sheol/Hades as a
**real but temporary** holding place: souls await the final judgment,
righteous separated from unrighteous (Luke 16:19-31, the great chasm).
**No purgatory** — Pentecostal soteriology is sharp on this; "to be
absent from the body, [is] to be present with the Lord" (2 Cor 5:8) for
the believer, and the dead in Christ rest until the resurrection.

## PF2e plane mapping

Maps to the **Boneyard** ferry zone — the pre-judgment intermediate
state. **Distinct from final Hell** ([[pentecostal:cosmology-gehenna]]),
**distinct from the Abyss** ([[pentecostal:cosmology-abyss]]) which is
the operational HQ of present-age demonic activity. Pharasma's vanilla
gatekeeping role reskins cleanly; the gatekeeping is delegated angelic
authority, not autonomous deity.

## Spiritual-warfare framing

Sheol is **not** a primary battleground in the present age — the war is
in the heavenlies and on earth ([[pentecostal:cosmology-abyss]]). The
chasm of Luke 16 is fixed; demons cannot retrieve souls from Abraham's
bosom, nor can the Cleric's *raise dead* poach them — every resurrection
is a soul recalled from rest by *YHWH's* permission, not an invasive
necromantic extraction. *Void*-trait spells are anathema for canonical
Pentecostal Clerics ([[pentecostal:class-cleric]]); they are the
adversary's parody.

## Campaign hooks

- 1 Peter 3:19 — Christ's preaching to "spirits in prison" — is a
  midtier setpiece for a Cleric who descends to Sheol on a deliverance
  mission (with explicit table buy-in; this is theologically loaded
  territory).
