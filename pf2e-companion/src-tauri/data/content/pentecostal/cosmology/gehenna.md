# Gehenna — the lake of fire

Final-state, eschatological judgment: the lake of fire of Revelation 20,
reserved for the dragon, the beast, the false prophet, Death and Hades
themselves, and the unrepentant. The Pentecostal lens emphasizes the
**verdict-rendered** character of Gehenna — final, conscious, eternal —
while distinguishing it sharply from the **present-age battlefield**
([[pentecostal:cosmology-abyss]]).

## PF2e plane mapping

Maps to **Hell** in vanilla PF2e nomenclature. Sanctification of
inhabitants: nearly all unholy. *Banishment* sends infernal creatures
here as their original plane.

## Spiritual-warfare framing — the load-bearing point

**Gehenna is not where the present war is fought.** This is the
Pentecostal cosmological distinctive: the lake of fire is *future*
disposal, not *current* operations. The dragon, the beast, and the
false prophet are not yet in the lake of fire (Rev 19-20 puts that at
the eschaton). The present-age war is over the
[[pentecostal:cosmology-abyss]] — territorial spirits contending for
geography and souls *now*. Players should encounter Gehenna as the
*coming* sentence, the eschatological backstop; they encounter the
**Abyss** as the actual operational theatre.

This separation matters mechanically: a Cleric's *banishment* sends
demons back to the Abyss, not to Gehenna; the lake of fire is not a
plane PCs travel to mid-campaign except in apocalyptic visions or as a
Daniel-style dream/foretaste.

## Campaign hooks

- A capstone vision of the great white throne (Rev 20:11-15) as the
  campaign's climactic Oracle revelation.
- **Avoid using Gehenna as a routine planar destination.** Reserve it
  for the eschaton; it is structurally a one-way gate post-judgment.
