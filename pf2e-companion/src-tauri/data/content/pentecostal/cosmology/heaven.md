# Heaven — the Throne Room, the Third Heaven

The throne of Revelation 4-5; the third heaven of 2 Cor 12:2. The
Pentecostal lens reads the New Testament's three-heavens cosmology
plainly: the **first heaven** is the sky/atmosphere, the **second
heaven** is the celestial / "heavenlies" of Eph 6:12 — the operational
battle zone where territorial spirits contend — and the **third heaven**
is the throne room proper, the dwelling of God.

This three-tier reading is the load-bearing distinctive vs other lenses:
the **second heaven is a war zone**. Daniel 10's "prince of the kingdom
of Persia" detains Gabriel for twenty-one days *in the second-heaven
airspace* between heaven and earth. Prayer, fasting, and proclamation
are weapons in that air war (2 Cor 10:3-5).

## PF2e plane mapping

Maps to the **Empyrean** / Heaven plane — the third-heaven throne is the
upper celestial reach. The **second-heaven** is *not* a separate Paizo
plane; treat it as **the spiritual layer overlaid on the Material
Plane** — perceptible via *see invisibility*, *true seeing*, the
discernment-of-spirits charism, and Oracle visions.

Per [[pentecostal:yhwh]] anathema (grieve the Spirit; deny the gifts),
*void*-trait spells do not function in the throne-room proper, and
Oracle visions in heaven are **uncursed** — a moment of relief from the
cursebound burden, narratively framed (cf. 2 Cor 12:4: "caught up into
paradise, and heard unspeakable words").

## Campaign hooks

- A high-level "Daniel 10 fast" downtime activity that, after twenty-one
  days, breaks a territorial-spirit encounter offstage — converting
  out-of-game prayer-and-fasting fiction into a real mechanical
  resource. (Optional; do not require real-world fasting.)
- A Pauline rapture-of-Paul scene (2 Cor 12) as a midtier vision quest.
