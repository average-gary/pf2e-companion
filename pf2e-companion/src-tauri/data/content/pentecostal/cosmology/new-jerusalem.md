# the New Jerusalem — the eschaton

The city descending out of heaven; the new heavens and new earth in
which righteousness dwells. The Pentecostal lens is **broadly
premillennial** in its dominant strain (though not universally so —
amillennial Pentecostals exist), and many tables will run a campaign
that explicitly culminates in the Lord's return in glory (1 Thess 4:16),
the millennial reign, the white-throne judgment ([[pentecostal:cosmology-gehenna]]),
and the descent of the New Jerusalem.

## PF2e plane mapping

There is no vanilla PF2e plane that maps cleanly. The New Jerusalem is a
**campaign endgame state** — the post-eschaton fusion of the Material
Plane with [[pentecostal:cosmology-heaven]]. A 20+ level adventure path
ending with the descent of the city, or with the marriage supper of the
Lamb, is the natural shape of a Pentecostal-lens capstone.

## Mechanical notes

- Within the New Jerusalem, *heal* is at-will and *resurrect* is
  unnecessary — there is no death (Rev 21:4).
- **There is no temple** (Rev 21:22) — God and the Lamb are its temple.
- The river of life and the tree of life are narrative-tier resolution,
  not commodity loot. Grant them as story payoff.
- **Charisms persist or transcend?** 1 Cor 13:8-10 ("when that which is
  perfect is come, then that which is in part shall be done away")
  applies — tongues and prophecy *cease* in the eschaton because they
  are no longer needed. This is the **only context** in which a
  Pentecostal-lens game treats charisms as ceasing; their cessation is
  *eschatological*, not *post-apostolic*.

## Spiritual-warfare framing

The eschaton is **war ended**. The dragon is in the lake of fire
([[pentecostal:cosmology-gehenna]]); the Abyss is closed
([[pentecostal:cosmology-abyss]]); the bound spirits of
[[pentecostal:cosmology-tartarus]] have been judged. This is the
*resolution* of the campaign-long spiritual-warfare arc — the players
are present at its conclusion, not its continuation.
