# Daniel the Prophet — exemplar of visions and discernment

Daniel is the Pentecostal pattern for **prophetic vision and the word of
knowledge** (1 Cor 12:8, 10). He reads dreams that the king's wise men
cannot (Dan 2); he sees the four-beast vision (Dan 7); his three-week
fast in Daniel 10 is the locus classicus of **territorial-spirit
warfare** in the Old Testament — Gabriel arrives delayed because the
"prince of the kingdom of Persia" (a hostile principality) withstood him
(Dan 10:13). This single passage is foundational for the Pentecostal
spiritual-warfare reading of [[pentecostal:cosmology-abyss]].

> Daniel is an **exemplar**, not a venerated saint.

## Cleric / Oracle exemplar

Cleric: **Knowledge** or **Truth** domain. Oracle: **Cosmos** mystery —
the apocalyptic visionary archetype, a perfect cursebound fit (Dan 8:27,
"I was sick certain days"). Mechanical hooks: *augury*, *foresight*, and
*vision* as charisms; *true seeing* / *see invisibility* as the
discernment-of-spirits gift; intercessory fasting as a downtime activity
that anchors the next prophecy. The Dan 10 "prince of Persia" trope is
the textbook setup for a campaign in which the party's prayers
**actually shift the angelic battle lines** offstage.
