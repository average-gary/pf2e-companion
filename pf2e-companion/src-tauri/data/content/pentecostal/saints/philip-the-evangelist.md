# Philip the Evangelist — exemplar of evangelism and supernatural transport

Philip — distinct from Philip the apostle — is the Pentecostal pattern
figure for **evangelism in the power of the Spirit**. In Samaria he
preaches Christ with attendant signs (Acts 8:6-7); on the Gaza road he
is supernaturally directed to the Ethiopian eunuch and, after the
baptism, "the Spirit of the Lord caught Philip away" (Acts 8:39) — the
Bible's clearest case of **bodily translation by the Spirit**. His four
unmarried daughters prophesied (Acts 21:9), the only New Testament
named-women charismatic team.

> Philip is an **exemplar**, not a venerated saint.

## Cleric / Champion exemplar

Cleric: **Truth** or **Travel** domain — the Spirit-led missionary.
Champion: cause **Justice** flavored as proclamation (the Acts 17 /
Acts 8 evangelist). Mechanical hooks: *dimension door* and *teleport*
reskinned as the Spirit-translation charism (Acts 8:39 is the
proof-text); *tongues* and *comprehend languages* as the Acts-2
charism in field operation; the eunuch's chariot scene as a roleplay
template — "do you understand what you are reading?" as the
Spirit-prompted opening line.
