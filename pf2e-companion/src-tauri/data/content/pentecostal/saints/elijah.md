# Elijah the Tishbite — exemplar of the gift of miracles

The pattern figure for the **gift of miracles** (1 Cor 12:10): fire from
heaven on Carmel, the widow's son raised, the jar of meal that did not
fail, the chariot of fire at the end. James 5:17 explicitly frames
Elijah as the *imitable* prophet — "a man of like passions as we are,
and he prayed earnestly" — the load-bearing Pentecostal proof-text for
continuationism: if Elijah's miracles came by *prayer*, not by
techne, then the same prayer is open to the church.

> Elijah is an **exemplar**, not a venerated saint. Tables imitate
> Elijah's bold petition; they do not pray *to* him.

## Cleric / Oracle exemplar

Cleric: **Tempest** or **Zeal** domain — Elijah is the storm-and-fire
prophet. Oracle: **Tempest** or **Battle** mystery, with Carmel-style
sign-acts as the cursebound burden ([[pentecostal:class-oracle]]).
Mechanical hooks: *fireball* / *flame strike* reskinned as Carmel-fire;
*raise dead* (the widow's son); *control weather* (the three-and-a-half
year drought of 1 Kgs 17:1, James 5:17). The *banishment* of YHWH's
default spell list ([[pentecostal:yhwh]]) is an Elijahic charism —
Carmel was a deliverance encounter against the prophets of Baal.
