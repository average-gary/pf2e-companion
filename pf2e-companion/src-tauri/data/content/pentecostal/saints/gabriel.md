# Gabriel the Archangel

The herald — interpreter of visions to Daniel, announcer of John's birth
to Zechariah, announcer of Christ's incarnation to Mary. The second of
the two archangels named in the Protestant 66-book canon. The
Pentecostal lens treats Gabriel's role as **the model for the prophetic
charism** (1 Cor 12:10): true news, faithfully delivered, regardless of
cost to the messenger.

> Gabriel is an **exemplar**, not a venerated saint. We imitate his
> faithfulness; we do not invoke his name.

## Cleric / Oracle exemplar

A Cleric patterned on Gabriel takes the **Knowledge** or **Truth**
domain. An Oracle patterned on Gabriel takes the **Cosmos** or **Lore**
mystery, reframed as the prophetic calling
([[pentecostal:class-oracle]]). Edict overlay: deliver true news at any
cost; interpret visions and dreams faithfully; refuse to be a herald of
comfortable lies. Mechanical hooks: *augury* and *foresight* as the
prophetic word-of-knowledge charism in operation; *sending* as the
Spirit-borne herald's voice.
