# Michael the Archangel

The chief warrior of the heavenly host. Michael is the archangel who
contends with the dragon (Rev 12:7) and stands for the people of God
(Dan 12:1). The Pentecostal lens classes him with the **two named
archangels of the 66-book canon** (Michael, Gabriel) — central, not
peripheral, to spiritual-warfare cosmology
([[pentecostal:cosmology-abyss]]).

> **Note on terminology.** Michael is *not* a "saint" in the venerated
> sense; the Pentecostal lens does not invoke saints, light candles, or
> address prayers to angels (cf. Col 2:18; Rev 22:8-9). He is an
> **exemplar** — a scriptural figure whose vocation a Champion or Cleric
> may *imitate* but never *invoke*.

## Champion exemplar

A Champion patterned on Michael takes cause **Justice** (paladin) or
**Liberation** (liberator). Edict overlay: contend against principalities
and powers in the heavenlies (Eph 6:12); refuse the dragon's contracts;
break ranks with no brother in the deliverance line. Mechanical hooks:
Champion's Aura framed as the Spirit's anointing for warfare (1 Sam
16:13); *holy light* and *banishment* as on-the-field charisms.
