# Paul the Apostle — exemplar of tongues, healing, and deliverance

Paul is the **paradigm Pentecostal exemplar** because he names himself a
tongues-speaker (1 Cor 14:18) *and* a worker of miracles (Acts 19:11)
*and* a deliverance-minister (Acts 16:18) *and* a charismata theologian
(1 Cor 12 itself). His ministry compresses the entire 1 Cor 12 list into
a single career, while 2 Cor 12's "thorn in the flesh" preserves the
theological humility — charisms are gift, not entitlement.

> Paul is an **exemplar**, not a venerated saint.

## Cleric / Champion / Oracle exemplar

Cleric: **Truth**, **Healing**, or **Zeal** domain — the apostolic
all-rounder. Champion: cause **Liberation** (the Philippi exorcism;
Acts 16:18) or **Justice** (Areopagus preaching, Acts 17). Oracle:
**Lore** (the rabbi-scholar) or **Battle** (the Acts 19 sons-of-Sceva
deliverance encounter as the operational template). Mechanical hooks:
*tongues* as a literal, in-fiction Acts 2 charism (not just a utility
spell); *banishment* and *holy light* against territorial spirits;
*heal* applied to handkerchiefs and aprons (Acts 19:12) for narrative
flavor. The "thorn" maps cleanly to an Oracle's progressing curse —
*charisma without health-and-wealth triumphalism* is a Pentecostal
distinctive worth foregrounding.
