# Thaumaturge — the deliverance minister

**The Thaumaturge is an awkward fit for the Pentecostal lens** —
deliberately so. The Lewisian and Catholic-leaning lenses read the
Thaumaturge's nine implements as **relics** (the chalice as the Holy
Grail tradition, etc.). The Pentecostal lens is **anti-relic**: relic-
veneration, sacramental objects, and the broader category of
"consecrated stuff that channels grace" sit very awkwardly with
Pentecostal sensibilities, which keep the source of power firmly *in
the Spirit, not in matter*.

The reskin therefore reframes: the Thaumaturge is a **deliverance
minister wielding biblical prayers and proclamations as implements** —
the Eph 6:10-18 armor-of-God image taken concretely. The implements are
**verbal-and-symbolic, not material-relic**.

## Implement reskin table — verbal/symbolic, not relic

| PF2e implement | Pentecostal reskin | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Tome | the Bible itself, opened and proclaimed in faith | Heb 4:12; 2 Tim 3:16 |
| Lantern | "thy word is a lamp" — the Spirit's illumination over a place | Ps 119:105 |
| Weapon | the "sword of the Spirit, which is the word of God" | Eph 6:17 |
| Bell | the trumpet / shofar of proclamation; spiritual-warfare warning | Joel 2:1; 1 Cor 14:8 |
| Wand | the staff lifted in prayer (Moses at Rephidim, Ex 17:11) | Ex 17:11 |
| Mirror | discernment of spirits — seeing the unseen accurately | 1 Cor 12:10; 13:12 |
| Amulet | a verse memorized over the heart (NOT a phylactery) | Deut 6:6, 8 (phrase-on-the-heart, not literal object-veneration) |
| Regalia | the Eph 6 armor of God, in toto — non-physical | Eph 6:11-17 |
| Chalice | the cup of communion as memorial / proclamation, NOT eucharistic-real-presence | 1 Cor 11:25-26 |

## What this is NOT

- **Not a Catholic relic-pilgrim**. The Pentecostal Thaumaturge does
  not collect relics, kiss bones, light candles before icons, or carry
  consecrated objects as channels of grace. If the table wants
  relic-flavor, switch to the Lewisian or (pending) Catholic lens.
- **Not a sacramentalist**. The chalice is *memorial*, not *real
  presence*. The Pentecostal lens is firmly in the memorial /
  proclamation tradition (Zwinglian) on the Lord's Supper.

## Acts 19:11-12 as the operational template

"And God wrought special miracles by the hands of Paul: so that from his
body were brought unto the sick handkerchiefs or aprons, and the
diseases departed from them, and the evil spirits went out of them."
This is **the canonical Pentecostal proof-text** for "anointed objects"
— but note the verse's careful theology: the power is by the **hands of
Paul**, not in the cloths themselves. The Thaumaturge's implements
function the same way: they are *contact points* for the Spirit's
work, not autonomous channels.

## Spiritual-warfare framing

The Pentecostal Thaumaturge is best built as a **deliverance specialist
auxiliary to the Cleric**: implements support the discernment-and-
banishment loop. Mirror + Tome + Weapon is the signature build.
