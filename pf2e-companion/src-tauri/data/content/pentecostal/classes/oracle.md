# Oracle — the prophet under the Spirit

If the Cleric is the Pentecostal lens's anointed *minister*, the Oracle
is the Pentecostal lens's anointed *prophet*. The PF2e Remaster
Oracle's **cursebound** mechanic — power that comes with progressively
worsening physical or social cost — is structurally identical to the
prophet experience under both old covenant (Jer 20:9, "fire shut up in
my bones") and new (Acts 21:9-11; 1 Cor 14:32).

The Pentecostal lens *intensifies* this fit. Joel 2:28 ("your sons and
daughters shall prophesy") + Acts 2:17 (Peter's explicit
"this-is-that" identification) means **prophecy is normative in the
present age**, and the Oracle is its mechanical expression. The four
prophesying daughters of Philip ([[pentecostal:philip-the-evangelist]])
are the New Testament's named-women charismatic team and a direct
template.

## Build recipe

1. **Mystery** (reflavor as "calling"):
   - **Lore** mystery → scribal prophet (Daniel,
     [[pentecostal:daniel]])
   - **Battle** mystery → herald of judgment + deliverance
     (Elijah on Carmel, [[pentecostal:elijah]])
   - **Cosmos** mystery → apocalyptic visionary (Daniel 7-12; the Acts
     21 Agabus type)
   - **Tempest** mystery → fire-and-storm prophet
     ([[pentecostal:elijah]])
   - **Life** mystery → resurrection-and-revival prophet
2. **Curse** = the prophetic burden — physical / social cost that
   intensifies. Jeremiah's weeping. Hosea's marriage. Paul's "thorn"
   (2 Cor 12:7). 2 Cor 12:7 is the explicit Pentecostal proof-text that
   high charisma walks with sustained physical limitation —
   *charismatic without health-and-wealth triumphalism.*
3. **Deity**: [[pentecostal:yhwh]]. The mystery is the calling, not a
   second deity slot.

## Charisms framing

The Oracle's spell list is read as the **prophecy + word-of-knowledge +
discernment-of-spirits** axis of [[pentecostal:reference-charismata]].
*augury*, *foresight*, *vision*, *true seeing*, *see invisibility* are
the Oracle's bread and butter and are described as charismatic
manifestations — not as "psychic" or "occult" effects.

## The DragonRaid caveat (mandatory)

**Do not require the player to speak in tongues, prophesy at the table,
or recite scripture to cast.** The cursebound burden is mechanical; the
prophetic flavor is descriptive. A player who is comfortable describing
prophetic visions in-character may; a player who is not should not be
penalized.

## Spiritual-warfare integration

The Oracle's discernment is a load-bearing party asset against
territorial-spirit encounters ([[pentecostal:cosmology-abyss]]). The
Daniel-10 trope — twenty-one-day fast, prophetic vision unlocks the
strategic intel — makes the Oracle's curse and the party's downtime
mechanically connected. A GM can use the Oracle's progressing curse as
the campaign's ticking clock.
