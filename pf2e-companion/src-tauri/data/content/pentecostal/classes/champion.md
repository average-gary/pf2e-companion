# Champion — the anointed evangelist warrior

The Pentecostal Champion is **the anointed evangelist warrior**: the
Acts 10:38 figure ("anointed with the Holy Spirit and with power, who
went about doing good and healing all that were oppressed of the
devil"). The Remaster Champion's sanctification + cause + edict/anathema
framework is a perfect mechanical fit; the Pentecostal lens overlays
**the Spirit's anointing** as the in-fiction explanation of the
Champion's Aura, and **deliverance ministry** as the operational
context.

## Build recipe

1. **Deity**: [[pentecostal:yhwh]].
2. **Sanctification**: holy.
3. **Cause** = the Acts-10:38 / Luke-4:18 grid:
   - **Justice** (paladin) — proclamation against oppression
   - **Liberation** (liberator) — Luke 4:18, "freedom for the captives"
     (deliverance ministry as cause; the Acts 16 Philippi exorcism is
     the textbook setup)
   - **Mercy/Redemption** (redeemer) — healing the oppressed of the
     devil; the load-bearing Pentecostal cause for a healing-focused
     Champion
4. **Edicts/anathema**: lift verbatim from [[pentecostal:yhwh]] —
   "obey the Spirit's leading; lay hands on the sick; proclaim freedom
   to captives; pursue holiness" / "grieve the Spirit; deny the gifts;
   profane the gathered church; love money."

## Charisms framing — the load-bearing distinctive

The Champion's Aura is **the Spirit's anointing** (1 Sam 16:13; Acts
10:38) — not an aura of force, not a cosmetic glow, but the literal
in-fiction *chrism*. *Lay on Hands* is the gift of healings (1 Cor
12:9). Sense Holiness / Sense Unholiness is **discernment of spirits**
(1 Cor 12:10) — the ability to perceive what kind of spirit is
operating in a given situation. *Holy* damage is the Spirit's
sanctifying presence wounding the demonic
([[pentecostal:cosmology-abyss]]).

## Deliverance-ministry framing

A Pentecostal Champion is **expected** to perform deliverance under
field conditions. The Liberation cause is most explicit, but every
Champion-cause variant should expect to face territorial spirits and
demonic possession encounters as routine campaign content. The Acts 19
sons-of-Sceva story (Acts 19:13-17) is the cautionary counterpoint:
deliverance without genuine anointing fails badly. Cause this in-fiction
when an NPC tries to invoke power without faith.

## The DragonRaid caveat

**Do not require real-world prayer or tongue-speaking to activate
Champion abilities.** Mechanical surface is identical to vanilla PF2e
Champion; flavor is the anointing.
