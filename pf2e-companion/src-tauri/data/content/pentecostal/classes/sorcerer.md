# Sorcerer — Angelic bloodline ONLY

The Pentecostal lens is the most restrictive of any lens for the
Sorcerer class. The hybrid #1+#2 Charism + Lewisian framework that
underwrites the **Lewisian** Sorcerer doesn't fully apply here —
Pentecostal sensibilities are sharper on the Deut 18 / Acts 8 / Acts 19
boundary, and "bloodline as inherited supernatural power" works *only*
when the bloodline is unambiguously aligned with YHWH.

**Verdict**: the Angelic bloodline is the **only** unambiguously
playable Sorcerer for canonical PCs. All other bloodlines are
**villain-side** — territorial spirits operating through human lineages
([[pentecostal:cosmology-abyss]]) — or are cut.

## Bloodline verdict table

| Bloodline | Pentecostal verdict | Reskin |
|---|---|---|
| **Angelic** | **Ship — the *only* canonical PC option** | Spirit-anointed lineage; "before I formed you in the womb I knew you" (Jer 1:5) — power is hereditary access to charisms, identical in source to the Cleric |
| **Imperial** | Ship (cautious) | Royal Davidic / messianic lineage; flag for table approval |
| Aberrant | **Cut or villain-only** | Reads as Watcher-corrupted; tragic-arc framing only |
| Wyrmblood | **Cut or villain-only** | Leviathan/dragon descent reads too close to Rev 12 dragon-line for comfort |
| Genie | **Cut** | Spirit-of-the-elements bloodline reads as territorial-spirit pact |
| Psychopomp | **Cut** | Death-attendant lineage reads as necromantic; void-trait concerns |
| **Demonic** | **Cut** (NPC antagonist only) | Explicit demon-blood; territorial spirit operating in the family line |
| **Diabolic** | **Cut** (NPC antagonist only) | Lucifer-fall hierarchy lineage |
| **Hag** | **Cut** | Mediumistic / Endor-line; Deut 18 forbidden ground |
| **Shadow** | **Cut** | Sheol-haunted; void-trait concerns |
| **Undead** | **Cut hard** | Void trait is anathema for canonical Pentecostal magic |

## The Acts 16:16 antagonist hook

The slave girl with the "spirit of divination" (Acts 16:16-18) is the
**operational template for the villain Sorcerer in the Pentecostal
lens**: a person under demonic spiritual gifting that mimics charismatic
power. The Pentecostal lens calls these *counterfeit charisms* — they
are real, they are powerful, and they are demonically sourced. PCs who
encounter such NPCs should resolve them via deliverance, not combat
where possible. The Acts 19 sons-of-Sceva story is the *negative*
template: deliverance attempted without genuine anointing fails badly.

## Magic-theology framing

The Pentecostal Sorcerer is closer to **pure approach #1 (Charism)** in
[[magic-theology-approaches]] than the Lewisian hybrid. Sorcery here is
*gift*, not technique — but bloodline-as-mechanism is theologically
loaded enough that only the Angelic bloodline survives the cut. The
practical effect: **the Pentecostal Sorcerer plays mechanically like a
Divine spontaneous caster**, narratively framed as a hereditary version
of the same charismatic anointing the Cleric receives by ministry call.
