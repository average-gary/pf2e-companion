# Cleric — the anointed minister of charisms

The Pentecostal Cleric is the load-bearing reskin of the pack. The PF2e
Remaster Cleric framework (doctrine + edicts/anathema + divine font)
translates *cleanly* into Pentecostal ministry; the load is entirely
flavor. **Every YHWH-aligned Cleric is presumed to manifest charisms**
(see [[pentecostal:reference-charismata]]) — *heal* is a gift of
healings, *holy light* is a sign-and-wonder, *banishment* is deliverance.

## Build recipe

1. **Deity**: [[pentecostal:yhwh]].
2. **Doctrine**:
   - **Cloistered cleric** = pastor / Bible teacher / intercessor.
     Suited to the prayer-warrior archetype; pair with Daniel-style
     fasting downtime ([[pentecostal:daniel]]).
   - **Warpriest** = revivalist evangelist; the Acts 19 Paul archetype
     ([[pentecostal:paul]]) — preaching and deliverance in a single
     career.
3. **Divine Font**: **heal** (universally). *Harm* is reserved for
   explicitly fallen / counterfeit ministry — the Simon Magus / Acts 19
   sons-of-Sceva trope as villainous NPC framing. Canonical Pentecostal
   Clerics never take *harm*.
4. **Domains** ([[pentecostal:yhwh]]'s set): primary Healing, Truth,
   Zeal, Vigil; alternate Knowledge, Family, Confidence.
5. **Anathema**: *void*-trait spells are anathema for the canonical
   Pentecostal Cleric. The void trait is the adversary's parody of
   resurrection.

## Charisms framing — the load-bearing distinctive

Each spell-cast is **described in-fiction as the manifestation of a
specific 1 Cor 12 gift**:

- *heal* / *cleanse affliction* / *sound body* → gift of healings
- *bless* / *guidance* → word of wisdom or word of knowledge
- *holy light* / *banishment* → workings of miracles
- *augury* / *foresight* → prophecy
- *see invisibility* / *true seeing* → discerning of spirits
- *tongues* / *comprehend languages* → tongues + interpretation

See [[pentecostal:reference-charismata]] for the full mapping.

## The DragonRaid caveat (mandatory)

**Do not require players to pray, recite scripture, or speak in tongues
at the table to cast spells.** Mechanizing piety as input is the
DragonRaid trap and breaks the table for both believing and unbelieving
players. The mechanical surface is identical to vanilla PF2e Cleric;
the *flavor* is "I lay hands on him and the Lord heals through me."
Description, not invocation.

## Spiritual-warfare integration

A Pentecostal Cleric is the party's deliverance-ministry primary. *See
invisibility* / *true seeing* + *banishment* is the standard
two-action-type-combo against territorial-spirit encounters
([[pentecostal:cosmology-abyss]]). The party should expect at least one
demon-encounter per level by GM design — this lens runs hot on
spiritual-warfare content.
