# the New Jerusalem — the eschaton

The city descending out of heaven from God; the new heavens and new
earth in which righteousness dwells. Catholic eschatology (CCC
§1042–1050) affirms restoration, not annihilation: heaven and earth
are not destroyed and replaced but renewed, joined, and become the
dwelling of God with mortals. The lake of fire receives Death and
Hades themselves ([[catholic:cosmology-gehenna]]). The marriage
supper of the Lamb (Rev 19:9) is the eternal liturgy — the Eucharist
revealed in its full reality.

## PF2e plane mapping

There is no vanilla PF2e plane that maps cleanly. **The New Jerusalem
is a campaign endgame state** — the post-eschaton fusion of the
Material Plane with [[catholic:cosmology-heaven]]. A 20+ level
adventure path that ends with the descent of the city (or with the
players present at the marriage supper of the Lamb) is the natural
shape of a Catholic campaign's capstone.

## Mechanical notes

- Within the New Jerusalem, *Heal* is at-will and *Resurrect* is
  unnecessary — there is no death.
- The river of life and the tree of life are encounter-tier loot for
  a capstone scene, not items to grind for. Grant them as narrative
  resolution, not commodities.
- **There is no temple** (Rev 21:22) — the Lord God Almighty and the
  Lamb are its temple. This is a meaningful break with vanilla PF2e
  high-level cosmology and worth signposting in setting bibles. The
  Catholic Eucharistic theology, which already identifies the Mass
  as participation-now in the eternal liturgy, frames this as
  fulfillment rather than rupture.
- [[catholic:cosmology-purgatory]] is emptied by the general
  resurrection — its denizens have all been purified and entered the
  city.
