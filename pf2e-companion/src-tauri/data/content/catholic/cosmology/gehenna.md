# Gehenna — the lake of fire, final hell

The Valley of Hinnom outside Jerusalem; the rubbish-and-corpse fire
that became the New Testament's name for the eschatological place of
punishment. The lake of fire of Revelation 20–21. The Catholic
Catechism (§1033–1037) defines hell as **the state of definitive
self-exclusion from communion with God and the blessed**. Final-state,
not intermediate; reserved for the unrepentant and for the dragon,
the beast, the false prophet, Death and Hades themselves.

## PF2e plane mapping

Maps to **Hell** (the lawful evil layer in vanilla PF2e nomenclature).
The Catholic lens preserves the distinction with [[catholic:cosmology-purgatory]]
sharply: purgatory is *purification of the saved*; hell is the
*chosen self-exclusion of the damned*. They are not points on a
spectrum.

Pride-fall fiends (the [[catholic:cosmology-abyss]]'s Lucifer-line
devils, in the Christian double-origin tradition) populate Gehenna's
courts and prisons. Catholic angelology preserves the named hierarchy
of fallen powers: Satan/Lucifer, Beelzebub (Mt 12:24), Mammon (Mt
6:24), Belial (2 Cor 6:15), Asmodeus (Tob 3:8 — full canonical force
in this lens).

## Mechanical notes

- Sanctification of inhabitants: nearly all unholy.
- *Banishment* sends infernal creatures here as their original plane.
- An adventurer crossing Gehenna alive is doing harrowing-of-hell
  work; see Christ's descent in [[catholic:cosmology-sheol]] for the
  canonical pattern. **Note**: Catholic theology distinguishes the
  *limbus patrum* (which Christ emptied) from final Gehenna (which is
  permanent).
