# the Abyss / *tehom* — the deep

The chaos-deep over which the Spirit hovered in Genesis 1:2, present
before the ordering of creation. In Revelation it is opened by a key,
it looses locusts, it swallows the dragon for a thousand years.
Distinct from final Hell ([[catholic:cosmology-gehenna]]) and distinct
from the specific pit of bound Watchers ([[catholic:cosmology-tartarus]]);
the Abyss is **the wider primordial deep**, both prison and
unmade-place.

## PF2e plane mapping

Maps to the **Abyss** in vanilla PF2e nomenclature. In the Catholic
double-origin tradition for fiends:

- **Devils** = Lucifer-fall hierarchy (Isa 14, Ezek 28, Rev 12) →
  courts of [[catholic:cosmology-gehenna]]; named: Lucifer/Satan,
  Beelzebub, Mammon, Belial, Asmodeus (the last from Tobit, fully
  canonical here).
- **Demons** = Watcher-fall hierarchy (1 Enoch tradition; demons as
  spirits of slain Nephilim per 1 En 15:8-9) → ride out from the
  Abyss; the [[catholic:cosmology-tartarus]] pit holds the named
  ringleaders; lesser shades roam the wider Abyss.
- **Daemons** = Revelation horsemen / personifications of plague,
  famine, death → lurk along the Abyss's edges.

## Campaign hooks

- Descents to the Abyss are *not* descents to final Hell. The water
  imagery of the deep (Job's Leviathan; the dragon-that-was-Pharaoh
  of Ezekiel 32) makes the Abyss a maritime-dungeon flavor distinct
  from Gehenna's furnace.
- The dragon's binding for a thousand years (Rev 20) is a
  campaign-scale hook — and it ends.
- Catholic exorcistic tradition (the Roman Ritual's *De Exorcismis*)
  treats the Abyss as a place demons are sent back to — flavor only;
  no real-world ritual at the table.
