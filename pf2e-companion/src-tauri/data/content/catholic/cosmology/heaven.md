# Heaven — the Throne Room, the Beatific Vision

Where the throne is. The throne room of Revelation 4–5; the third
heaven to which Paul was caught up (2 Cor 12:2); the sea of glass
before the throne; the four living creatures and the twenty-four
elders. The Catholic Catechism (§1023–1029) defines heaven as the
state of supreme and definitive happiness — the *beatific vision* —
in which the blessed see God face to face. Around the throne stand
the **communion of saints** (CCC §946–962): the Church Triumphant in
unbroken intercessory communion with the Church Militant on earth and
the Church Suffering in [[catholic:cosmology-purgatory]].

## PF2e plane mapping

Maps to the **Empyrean** / Heaven plane. Reskin Paizo's celestial
creature ladder onto the full **nine-rank Pseudo-Dionysian hierarchy**
per Aquinas's *Summa* I q.108:

- **First sphere (contemplative)**: Seraphim, Cherubim, Thrones —
  Solar / Empyrean tier; biblical-form Cherubim (four-faced, Ezek 1)
  not putti.
- **Second sphere (governance)**: Dominions, Virtues, Powers —
  Planetar / astral deva tier; Dominions order the spheres, Virtues
  administer miracles, Powers bind demons.
- **Third sphere (engaged)**: Principalities (nation-guardians, Dan
  10), Archangels ([[catholic:michael]] · [[catholic:gabriel]] ·
  [[catholic:raphael]] · [[catholic:uriel]]), Angels (guardian
  angels — the order most engaged with humans).

Per [[catholic:yhwh]] anathema, **necromantic and consult-the-dead
spells do not function here in their pagan mode** — but the
intercession of saints is not necromancy: it is liturgical prayer to
the Church Triumphant (CCC §956). A Cleric or Champion may petition a
named saint as part of normal ritual play.
