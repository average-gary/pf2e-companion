# Tartarus — the prison of the fallen Watchers

A specific place of binding referenced once in canonical Scripture (2
Peter 2:4): the deepest pit, where rebellious angels are "kept in
chains under darkness for the judgment of the great day." Jude 6
pairs with this passage. The 1 Enoch tradition — non-canonical to
Roman Catholic Scripture, but quoted in Jude 14–15 and influential on
patristic imagination — names the bound angels: Azazel under jagged
rocks in Dudâêl, Semjâzâ and his hosts under the hills for seventy
generations.

## PF2e plane mapping

Maps to the **Tartarus floor of the Abyss** (or to a sealed sub-layer
of Hell, depending on the campaign's geography). **Distinct from
[[catholic:cosmology-gehenna]]** — Gehenna is the lake-of-fire final
state of the unrepentant; Tartarus is the present-age binding of
specific named angelic offenders awaiting the day of judgment.
Distinct from the broader [[catholic:cosmology-abyss]] / *tehom* of
Genesis 1:2 and Revelation 9 which is a wider chaos-prison.

## Campaign hooks

- A high-level dungeon descent to recover a pre-Flood relic (a
  fragment of forbidden craft per 1 Enoch 8) is the classic Tartarus
  expedition. The Catholic GM should signpost 1 Enoch's status: the
  imagery is in the wiki, the canon is not.
- The forbidden-arts catalogue — the Watchers' teaching — is the
  primary in-fiction *fons* of demonic technique. PCs who recover it
  must decide: destroy, study, or wield.
