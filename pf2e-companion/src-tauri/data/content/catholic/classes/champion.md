# Champion — the Catholic Champion

The Remaster Champion is the **single biggest mechanical reskin win**
for a Catholic-themed game. Sanctification (holy / unholy / none) has
replaced alignment-locked causes; cause is decoupled from any axis-
grid; edicts/anathema attach to the deity. **Total custom-rule
footprint: zero.** Everything is flavor — and the Catholic lens
provides the richest patron roster in the genre.

## Build recipe

1. **Deity**: [[catholic:yhwh]] (or `sanctification: none` with the
   cause attached to a saint or to "the cause of righteousness" in the
   abstract).
2. **Sanctification**: holy.
3. **Cause** = **Beatitude** flavor:
   - **Justice** (paladin) — Mt 5:6, hunger and thirst for righteousness
   - **Liberation** (liberator) — Lk 4:18, proclaim freedom for the
     captives
   - **Mercy/Redemption** (redeemer) — Mt 5:7, blessed are the merciful
4. **Saint patron** (Catholic-distinctive): pick from the roster.
   Each saint overlay supplies additional edicts/anathema on top of
   the YHWH base. This is the richest Catholic mechanic — the named
   patron-saint Champion.

## Saint-patron variants (the major Catholic distinctive)

- **[[catholic:michael]]** — Justice/Liberation; warrior of the
  heavenly host.
- **[[catholic:raphael]]** — Redemption; healer and pilgrim's
  guardian.
- **[[catholic:mary-queen-of-heaven]]** — Mercy/Liberation; the
  *Magnificat* champion.
- **[[catholic:joseph]]** — Justice (the just man); guardian of the
  household.
- **[[catholic:peter]]** — Justice; the keys, the rock, the
  liberation-from-prison apostle.
- **[[catholic:francis-of-assisi]]** — Mercy; voluntary poverty,
  embrace of the leper.
- **[[catholic:james-greater]]** — Justice; the warrior-pilgrim of
  the Camino.
- **[[catholic:thomas]]** — Justice; doubt-into-confession.

(Plus the rest of the saints/ roster — every named saint here is a
playable Champion patron with edicts on file.)

## Honest gotchas

- **The Champion's Aura** is mechanically clean but flavor-loaded.
  Render as the "Glory" or "the Spirit's anointing" or, for a saint-
  patron Champion, as the saint's mantle or banner.
- **Sense Holiness / Sense Unholiness** feats reskin trivially as
  spiritual discernment (1 Cor 12:10) or as the Catholic *donum
  scientiae*.
- The **Holy / Unholy damage trait** is a real damage type now. NPCs
  should respect it — devils and demons take holy; angels take
  unholy. Catholic exorcistic flavor (sacramentals, holy water, the
  sign of the Cross) provides ample in-fiction texture.

## Sacramental anchors

A Catholic Champion's daily prep is naturally anchored to the
sacraments: Confession before missions, Mass on the day of, Anointing
in extremis. **Flavor only — never required as game mechanic.** The
DragonRaid trap (real-world prayer as casting requirement) is the
boundary: theology is content, not rules.
