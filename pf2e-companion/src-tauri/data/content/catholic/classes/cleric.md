# Cleric — the sacramental priest

The PF2e Remaster Cleric reflavors cleanly into Catholic-tradition
clergy: the doctrine + edicts/anathema framework is already deity-
customizable, so a Catholic Cleric is a **diocesan priest, religious
(monk/friar/nun), or bishop** depending on doctrine choice. The
sacramental backbone of Catholic ministry is the natural lens
distinctive — every sacramental rite is a ritual the Cleric NPC (or
PC, where appropriate) is uniquely empowered to perform.

## Build recipe

1. **Deity**: [[catholic:yhwh]], with optional saint patron from the
   [[catholic:michael]] · [[catholic:raphael]] · [[catholic:gabriel]] ·
   [[catholic:therese-of-lisieux]] · [[catholic:francis-of-assisi]]
   roster.
2. **Doctrine**:
   - **Cloistered cleric** = monastic / contemplative / scribal. The
     Carmelite, Benedictine, or Cistercian model. [[catholic:therese-of-lisieux]]
     is the iconic example.
   - **Warpriest** = chaplain to a Champion-led order (the Templars,
     the Hospitallers); or Old Testament priest-king like Melchizedek
     (Heb 7); or a missionary friar in dangerous territory (the
     Franciscan or Dominican mendicant tradition).
3. **Divine Font**: **heal** for canonical clergy; *harm* is reserved
   for explicitly fallen / apostate clerics.
4. **Anathema**: *void-trait spells* are anathema for canonical clergy
   (Catholic theology of the resurrection of the body forbids
   instrumentalizing death-trait magic).

## Sacramental ritual mechanics

The seven sacraments map to the PF2e ritual system as in-fiction
practices clergy NPCs perform — **flavor only**, no required
real-world recitation:

| Sacrament | Mechanical analog |
|---|---|
| **Baptism** | Atonement-style ritual, plus protection from undead/unholy on the newly baptized for a campaign-significant duration |
| **Confirmation** | Boost-style ritual; permanent flavor uplift on a charism |
| **Eucharist** | Daily prep anchor; "Real Presence" recovery boon at consecrated altars (in-fiction) |
| **Penance** | Atone (the existing PF2e ritual, deity-agnostic) |
| **Anointing of the Sick** | Restoration / Cleanse Affliction flavor |
| **Holy Orders** | One-time character milestone — a PC who takes orders unlocks ritual-specific mechanics |
| **Matrimony** | Tradition-specific bond between two PCs / NPCs; flavor-only buff to mutual aid |

**Sacramentals** (blessings, holy water, scapulars, the rosary, the
Brown Scapular, the Miraculous Medal) are the natural flavor for
[[catholic:class-thaumaturge]] implements rather than Cleric direct
mechanics.

## Honest gotchas

- **Charism vs technique.** The DragonRaid trap is making piety a
  mechanical input. **Do not require the player to recite real Latin,
  cross themselves, or pray.** The mechanical surface is the same as
  any PF2e Cleric; the flavor is "the gift of healing," "the word of
  knowledge," the charisms of 1 Cor 12.
- **Catholic continuationism**: this lens is *fully continuationist*.
  Charisms remain active in the Church; routine Cleric magic is
  theologically clean.

## Domain choices

Primary: Creation, Truth, Healing, Family. Alternate: Soul, Repose
(purgatory!), Zeal, Indulgence. (These are the [[catholic:yhwh]]
domain set; substitute or narrow for saint-patron Clerics —
[[catholic:raphael]] → Healing primary; [[catholic:michael]] → Zeal
primary; [[catholic:francis-of-assisi]] → Creation primary.)
