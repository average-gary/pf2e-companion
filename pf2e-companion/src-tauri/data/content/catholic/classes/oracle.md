# Oracle — the Catholic visionary

A surprise win. The PF2e Remaster Oracle's **cursebound** mechanic —
power that comes with progressively worsening physical or social cost
— is structurally identical not only to the OT prophet experience but
to the Catholic mystical tradition: stigmata, ecstasies, dark nights,
visions whose receipt costs the receiver. This lens preserves the OT
prophet flavor (always available as a build) and adds two
Catholic-distinctive Oracle archetypes: the **Marian visionary** and
the **mystic-doctor**.

> "If I say, 'I will not mention him, or speak any more in his name,'
> there is in my heart as it were a burning fire shut up in my bones,
> and I am weary with holding it in." — Jeremiah 20:9

## Build recipe

1. **Mystery** (reflavor as "calling"):
   - **Lore** mystery → scribal prophet (Daniel) or mystic-doctor
     ([[catholic:therese-of-lisieux]], Teresa of Avila, John of the
     Cross)
   - **Battle** mystery → herald of judgment (Elijah, Jeremiah's
     siege oracles)
   - **Cosmos** mystery → apocalyptic visionary ([[catholic:john]]
     of Patmos, Hildegard of Bingen) or **Marian visionary**
     (Bernadette of Lourdes, the children of Fatima, Juan Diego of
     Guadalupe)
   - **Tempest** mystery → storm-and-fire prophet (Elijah)
   - **Life** mystery → resurrection-and-revival prophet; or stigmatic
     mystic ([[catholic:francis-of-assisi]] of La Verna, Padre Pio)
2. **Curse**: render as the prophetic burden or mystical suffering —
   physical signs that intensify with use. The stigmata are the
   high-tier Catholic-specific manifestation; handle reverently and
   opt-in only.
3. **Deity**: [[catholic:yhwh]] — and **explicitly NOT a "mystery
   patron" in the polytheistic sense.** The mystery is your calling,
   not a deity slot. A Marian-visionary Oracle is given by Mary; the
   power flows from God through her intercession.

## The Marian visionary archetype

A Catholic-distinctive Oracle build. The **Cosmos** mystery, with
[[catholic:mary-queen-of-heaven]] as the in-fiction source of
visions, fits the approved Marian apparitions of Catholic tradition:
Bernadette at Lourdes (1858), the children at Fatima (1917), Juan
Diego at Guadalupe (1531). The vision-bearer is typically poor,
young, or unlettered — the *anawim* — and the messages have prophetic
weight.

## Honest gotchas

- **No rules change needed.** This is the cleanest reskin in the
  entire pack.
- **The prophetic / mystical burden is a feature, not a punishment.**
  Players opting into cursebound should know what they are signing on
  for. If your table doesn't like that mechanic, the Oracle isn't the
  right class for the prophet flavor — try [[catholic:class-cleric]]
  cloistered.
- **Stigmata flavor** is potent and edge-of-the-table; opt-in only.
