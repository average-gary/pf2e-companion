# Sorcerer — the Angelic bloodline canonical

The Sorcerer is the magic-saturated class with the cleanest reskin
**only because the Remaster shipped an Angelic bloodline as core**:
it grants the Divine tradition with explicit "descended from
celestials" flavor. That is the canonical Catholic Sorcerer: the
hereditary recipient of a charism, whose family carries it
generationally — the **Doctor-of-the-Church bloodline** flavor for a
Catholic campaign.

## Catholic-distinctive: the Doctor-of-the-Church bloodline

A new reskin angle the Lewisian pack does not develop. The Catholic
Church recognizes **37 Doctors** — saints whose theological insight
has been formally received by the universal Church (Aquinas, Augustine,
[[catholic:therese-of-lisieux]], Catherine of Siena, Hildegard of
Bingen, John of the Cross, etc.). An Angelic-bloodline Sorcerer
flavored as descending from (or carrying the charism of) a Doctor of
the Church is a highly specific Catholic build. The gift is
**mystical-theological insight**: spells flavored as illuminations
rather than incantations, with arcane / occult bloodline taxonomy
shifted to **divine** for any Doctor-flavored build.

## Magic-theology framing (Charism + Lewisian hybrid)

The hybrid framework treats Arcane / Occult / Primal traditions as
Lewisian conduits where the **player chooses the source**. The
Angelic sorcerer draws on the obedient-Watcher remainder; the
redeemed Aberrant sorcerer wrestles with a corrupted lineage; the
Demonic / Diabolic NPC sorcerer is the antagonist. Catholic theology
reads Aquinas's q.96 a.2 causal-vs-significatory test as the bright
line: an effect with natural causation (or received as a charism in
petition) is licit; an effect "by sign" through compact with fallen
spirits is illicit.

## Honest gotchas

- **Don't moralize at the table.** A Catholic-themed game in which
  the Sorcerer is constantly being lectured isn't fun. Set the lens,
  then let players inhabit their characters.
- **Hereditary charism vs sacramental ordination**. A canonical
  Catholic distinction worth keeping in flavor: Holy Orders confers
  an indelible character (sacramental priesthood); a hereditary
  charism is a different gift, neither lesser nor greater. A
  Sorcerer-Cleric multiclass is the in-fiction monk-with-mystical-
  visions trope.
