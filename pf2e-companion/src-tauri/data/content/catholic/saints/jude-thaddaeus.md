# Saint Jude Thaddaeus the Apostle

"Judas (not Iscariot)" — the apostle who at the Last Supper asks why
Christ will reveal himself to the disciples and not to the world (Jn
14:22). Author of the brief catholic epistle that cites Enoch and
warns against false teachers. Paired with [[catholic:simon-zealot]] in
mission to Persia. Catholic tradition makes him patron of impossible
or desperate cases — the saint to invoke when nothing else has
worked.

## Cleric / Champion patron

Cleric chooses **Vigil** or **Truth**. Champion takes cause **Mercy**
(the saint of last hope) or **Liberation**.

- **Edicts (additional)**: contend for the faith once delivered to
  the saints (Jude 3), keep watch with those whose cause is lost,
  call on the Most High for the desperate.
- **Anathema (additional)**: declare a cause hopeless, refuse to
  intercede for someone the world has written off.

## Mechanical hooks

- *Hope* (the spell), *Sanctuary*, and *Restoration* fit Jude's
  patronage-of-lost-causes.
- A novena to Jude is a flavor-only narrative beat — never a casting
  requirement.
