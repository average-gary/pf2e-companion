# Saint Matthew the Apostle and Evangelist

Levi son of Alphaeus, the tax collector called from the booth at
Capernaum. Author of the First Gospel — the Jewish-rooted, kingdom-
focused, Sermon-on-the-Mount gospel that opens the New Testament.
Patron of accountants, bankers, and (because of the conversion) of
all who think themselves beyond the reach of grace.

## Champion / Cleric patron

Champion takes cause **Mercy** — Matthew's call is the paradigmatic
"go and learn what this means: I desire mercy, not sacrifice" (Mt
9:13). Cleric chooses **Knowledge** or **Truth** for the
evangelist-scribe.

- **Edicts (additional)**: dine with sinners and tax-collectors,
  record the words of the Master faithfully, pursue the kingdom of
  heaven before treasure.
- **Anathema (additional)**: refuse a meal with a notorious sinner
  who is open to the gospel, falsify a record for personal gain.

- *Calm* and *Restoration* spells flavor as Matthean reconciliation
