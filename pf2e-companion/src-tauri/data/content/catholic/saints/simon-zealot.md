# Saint Simon the Zealot

One of the Twelve, called *ho Zelotes* — the Zealot — distinguishing
him from [[catholic:peter]] (Simon Peter). The epithet may name his
political-religious party of origin (the anti-Roman zealots of
first-century Judea) or simply his temperament. Tradition pairs him
with [[catholic:jude-thaddaeus]] in mission to Persia, where both
were martyred; their feast is shared on 28 October.

## Champion patron

Cause **Liberation** (the political-zealot turned gospel-zealot —
freedom redirected from earthly Rome to spiritual captivity).

- **Edicts (additional)**: redirect zeal from political vengeance to
  the kingdom of God, accompany a fellow-zealot in mission, refuse
  the easy victory of the sword.
- **Anathema (additional)**: take up the sword for nationalist
  vengeance, abandon a brother-in-mission.

## Mechanical hooks

- *Bless* and *Crusade*-style spells fit Simon's redirected zeal.
- The paired-with-Jude mission is the clean campaign hook for any
  party that wants two apostle-PCs.
