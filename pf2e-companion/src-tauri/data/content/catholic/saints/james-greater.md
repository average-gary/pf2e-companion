# Saint James the Greater

Son of Zebedee, brother of [[catholic:john]], one of the inner three
(with [[catholic:peter]] and John) who witnessed the Transfiguration,
the raising of Jairus's daughter, and Gethsemane. Christ named the
brothers *Boanerges*, "sons of thunder." First of the Twelve to be
martyred (beheaded by Herod Agrippa, Acts 12). Catholic tradition
sends him in legend to evangelize Iberia; his shrine at Santiago de
Compostela has been the great pilgrimage destination of Western
Europe for a millennium.

## Champion patron

Cause **Justice** (paladin) — the thunderous, zealous Champion. The
warrior-pilgrim aesthetic of the Camino is a cleanly playable backdrop.

- **Edicts (additional)**: walk the pilgrim's road, drink the cup of
  suffering when offered, bear arms only against the enemies of the
  poor.
- **Anathema (additional)**: refuse the pilgrim's hospitality, ask
  for the seats of glory ahead of others.
