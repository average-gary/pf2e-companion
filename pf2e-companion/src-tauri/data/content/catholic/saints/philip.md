# Saint Philip the Apostle

The apostle of John 14:8 — "Lord, show us the Father, and it is
enough for us." Christ's reply ("he who has seen me has seen the
Father") is one of the great Trinitarian self-revelations. Philip is
the apostle of inquiry, of "come and see" (Jn 1:46), of bringing the
Greeks to Christ (Jn 12:21). Tradition has him evangelizing Phrygia
and martyred at Hierapolis.

## Cleric / Oracle patron

Cleric chooses **Truth** or **Knowledge**. Oracle takes the **Lore**
mystery — Philip's "show us" is a paradigmatic vision-petition.

- **Edicts (additional)**: bring inquirers to Christ by saying "come
  and see," seek the face of the Father in all things, welcome
  outsiders to the table.
- **Anathema (additional)**: gatekeep the gospel from the curious,
  pretend to know the Father whom you have not sought.

## Mechanical hooks

- *True Seeing* and *Augury* fit Philip's vision-petitioning mode.
- Roleplay encounters featuring Greek-or-Gentile inquirers (the John
  12:20 trope) make natural Philip-aspected scenes.
