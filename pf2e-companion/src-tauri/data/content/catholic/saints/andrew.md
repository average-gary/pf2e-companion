# Saint Andrew the Apostle

The Protokletos — "first-called" — disciple of John the Baptist who
followed Jesus first and then brought his brother [[catholic:peter]].
Patron of Scotland, Russia, Greece, and Romania (each by tradition of
his evangelization). Martyred by tradition on the X-shaped saltire
cross at Patras.

## Champion patron

Cause **Liberation** (the proclaimer who brings others to Christ) or
**Justice**. Andrew's strength is missionary outreach — the patron of
the introducer, the one who brings the friend.

- **Edicts (additional)**: bring those near you to the Lord by name,
  share what little is on hand (the five loaves), refuse to seek
  precedence over your brothers.
- **Anathema (additional)**: keep the gospel to yourself, refuse a
  cross when one is set in your path.

## Mechanical hooks

- The saltire cross is a [[catholic:class-thaumaturge]] amulet (or
  weapon — the cross-staff trope).
- *Marvelous Multiplicity* and rituals of provisioning fit the
  five-loaves-and-two-fish narrative.
