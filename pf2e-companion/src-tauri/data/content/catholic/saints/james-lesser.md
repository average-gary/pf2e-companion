# Saint James the Lesser (the Just)

Bishop of Jerusalem, "brother of the Lord" (Gal 1:19), author of the
catholic epistle bearing his name. Presided at the Council of
Jerusalem (Acts 15) where he ruled that Gentile converts need not be
circumcised. Hegesippus reports he was called *Oblias* — "rampart of
the people" — and martyred by being thrown from the Temple parapet
and clubbed to death, kneeling and praying for his killers as he died.

## Cleric / Champion patron

Cleric chooses **Truth** or **Soul**. Champion takes cause **Mercy**
or **Justice** — James's epistle is the great New Testament call to
practical justice ("faith without works is dead").

- **Edicts (additional)**: be quick to listen and slow to speak, care
  for orphans and widows, refuse to show partiality between rich and
  poor.
- **Anathema (additional)**: curse those made in the image of God,
  speak peace while withholding bread, swear oaths beyond yes and no.

## Mechanical hooks

- *Sanctuary* and *Soothe* are James's pastoral-bishop spells.
- The Council-of-Jerusalem framework is a textbook diplomatic
  encounter for any setting that wants ecclesial politics.
