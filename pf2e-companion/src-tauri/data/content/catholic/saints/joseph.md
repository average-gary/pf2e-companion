# Saint Joseph, Spouse of the Virgin

The just man (Mt 1:19), foster-father of the Word made flesh, head of
the Holy Family. Joseph receives four dreams in Matthew, each obeyed
without recorded speech; he is the silent guardian, the carpenter of
Nazareth, named in 1870 patron of the Universal Church. Catholic
devotion adds patronage of fathers, workers, and the dying (he is
traditionally believed to have died with Jesus and Mary at his side).

## Champion / Cleric patron

Champion takes cause **Justice** (the Beatitudes' "blessed are the
just") or **Redemption** (protector). Cleric chooses the **Family** or
**Soul** domain.

- **Edicts (additional)**: provide for those in your charge, obey the
  voice of God in dreams and discernment, work with one's own hands.
- **Anathema (additional)**: abandon a family entrusted to you,
  withhold honest labor, presume to lead without listening.

## Mechanical hooks

- *Sanctuary* and *Augury* (dream-guidance) flavor cleanly as Joseph's
  intercession.
- A father-of-the-household NPC archetype maps to Joseph for any
  setting that wants a non-magical exemplar.
