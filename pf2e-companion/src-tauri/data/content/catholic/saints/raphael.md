# Saint Raphael the Archangel

The angel of Tobit — companion on the road, healer of the elder
Tobit's blindness, binder of the demon Asmodeus, the one who reveals
himself with "I am Raphael, one of the seven who stand before the
Lord" (Tob 12:15). Catholic tradition fully receives the deuterocanonical
witness: Tobit is canonical Scripture, not "apocrypha." Patron of
travelers, healers, and the engaged (after his role in Tobiah and
Sarah's marriage).

## Cleric / Champion patron

Cleric of Raphael chooses the **Healing** domain (primary). Champion
takes cause **Redemption** (redeemer) — his healing of Tobit's
blindness and Sarah's deliverance from Asmodeus map cleanly to the
redeemer's reactive aid.

- **Edicts (additional)**: tend to the sick and the wounded without
  partiality, accompany pilgrims on dangerous roads, bind demons that
  prey on the household.
- **Anathema (additional)**: refuse aid to a stranger, profit from
  another's affliction.

## Mechanical hooks

- *Heal* divine font is core. *Cleanse Affliction* (Remaster rename of
  *Remove Disease*) is Raphael's signature ritual.
- *Banishment* against household-spirit antagonists matches the
  Asmodeus-binding trope.
