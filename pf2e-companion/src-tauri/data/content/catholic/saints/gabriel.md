# Saint Gabriel the Archangel

The angel of the Annunciation. Gabriel interprets visions to Daniel,
announces the conception of John the Baptist to Zechariah, and brings
the *Ave gratia plena* to [[catholic:mary-queen-of-heaven]] at Nazareth.
The Catholic tradition names him patron of messengers, broadcasters,
and postal workers; the *Angelus* prayer (recited at 6 a.m., noon, and
6 p.m. across Christendom for centuries) commemorates his message.

## Cleric / Oracle patron

Gabriel is a herald, not a warrior; Cleric or Oracle fits better than
Champion. Cleric chooses the **Knowledge** or **Truth** domain; Oracle
takes the **Cosmos** or **Lore** mystery (reframed as the cosmic Word
announcing the times).

- **Edicts (additional)**: deliver true news at any cost, interpret
  visions and dreams faithfully, salute the Theotokos at the canonical
  hours.
- **Anathema (additional)**: speak as one who knows when one does not,
  silence a prophet, falsify a message.

## Mechanical hooks

- Bard composition spells reskin as "Gabriel's annunciation" —
  courageous anthem becomes the herald's call.
- *Sending* and *Augury* are the canonical Gabriel-aspected spells.
