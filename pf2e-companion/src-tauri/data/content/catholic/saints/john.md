# Saint John the Apostle and Evangelist

The disciple whom Jesus loved; son of Zebedee, brother of
[[catholic:james-greater]]. At the Cross he received Mary as mother
(Jn 19:27); on Patmos he received the Apocalypse. Author of the Fourth
Gospel, three epistles, and (by traditional ascription) Revelation.
The only apostle to die a natural death — and that in extreme old age
at Ephesus, having outlived all the others. Patron of theologians,
writers, and lovemakers (the agape kind).

## Cleric / Oracle patron

Strong Cleric or Oracle fit. Cleric chooses **Truth** or **Family**
(he is the Mother of God's adopted son). Oracle takes the **Cosmos**
mystery for the apocalyptic visionary, or **Lore** for the contemplative
beloved-disciple flavor.

- **Edicts (additional)**: love one another (it is enough; said often
  enough it is enough), bear witness to what you have seen and heard,
  remain faithful unto great age.
- **Anathema (additional)**: hate your brother (1 Jn 2:9), claim to
  know God while walking in darkness.

## Mechanical hooks

- *Foresight* and *Augury* are John's apocalyptic vision spells.
- A scroll-implement [[catholic:class-thaumaturge]] makes a strong
  Johannine pilgrim — the Apocalypse itself as relic.
