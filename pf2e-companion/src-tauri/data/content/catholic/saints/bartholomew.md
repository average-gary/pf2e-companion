# Saint Bartholomew the Apostle

Identified by Catholic tradition with Nathanael of John 1, the
"Israelite without guile" whom Jesus saw under the fig tree before
[[catholic:philip]] called him. Tradition has him evangelizing
Armenia and martyred there by being flayed alive — Michelangelo paints
him in the Sistine Last Judgment holding his own skin. Patron of
tanners, leatherworkers, and the persecuted.

## Champion / Cleric patron

Champion takes cause **Justice** with a Bartholomean stoicism: the
guileless witness who endures. Cleric chooses **Truth**.

- **Edicts (additional)**: speak without guile, bear witness under
  duress without recanting, remain present to those whose suffering
  is invisible.
- **Anathema (additional)**: dissemble to save one's skin (literally,
  in his case), trade truth for safety.

## Mechanical hooks

- The flaying-knife as Thaumaturge weapon implement is the rare-but-
  powerful relic — handle with care; some tables will find it grim.
- *Resist Death* / *Endure* spells flavor cleanly as Bartholomean
  endurance.
