# Saint Paul the Apostle

Saul of Tarsus, persecutor turned apostle to the Gentiles, struck blind
on the Damascus road and converted by direct vision of the risen
Christ (Acts 9). Author of thirteen New Testament epistles, founder of
churches across Asia Minor and Greece, caught up to the third heaven
(2 Cor 12). Patron of missionaries, theologians, and those persecuted
for the faith. Traditionally martyred at Rome under Nero alongside
[[catholic:peter]].

## Champion / Cleric / Oracle patron

Champion takes cause **Liberation** (the gospel "to the Jew first and
also to the Greek"). Cleric chooses **Truth** or **Knowledge**. Oracle
fits well — the thorn in the flesh is a textbook **Cursebound**
mechanic, and the third-heaven vision is paradigmatic Cosmos mystery.

- **Edicts (additional)**: preach the gospel in season and out, suffer
  hardship as a good soldier of Christ, defend the unity of the Body
  across cultural lines.
- **Anathema (additional)**: silence the gospel for fear of authorities,
  boast in oneself rather than in Christ crucified.

## Mechanical hooks

- *Bless*, *Banishment* (Acts 16 jailer), and the Oracle's curse
  signature are core to a Pauline build.
- A Pauline tome is the cleanest [[catholic:class-thaumaturge]]
  implement — the epistles themselves.
