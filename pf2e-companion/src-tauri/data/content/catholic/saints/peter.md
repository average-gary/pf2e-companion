# Saint Peter the Apostle

Simon son of Jonah, the rock on whom Christ builds his Church (Mt
16:18), keeper of the keys of the kingdom, first bishop of Rome. He is
the apostle of the great confession, the denial-and-restoration arc
("do you love me?" three times), the Pentecost preacher, the prisoner
freed by an angel (Acts 12). Patron of fishermen, popes, locksmiths,
and penitents.

## Champion / Cleric patron

Champion takes cause **Justice** (with strong Liberation overlay,
given the Acts 12 prison-break). Cleric chooses the **Truth** or
**Vigil** domain.

- **Edicts (additional)**: feed Christ's sheep, confess Christ before
  rulers, weep for one's own denials and rise.
- **Anathema (additional)**: deny Christ to save one's skin, withhold
  the keys (mercy, absolution) from the penitent.

## Mechanical hooks

- *Knock*, *Heal*, and *Banishment* (the prison-angel trope) flavor
  as Petrine miracles. Peter's shadow healing the sick (Acts 5:15) is
  the classic *Aura of Faith*-style narrative beat.
- The keys are a natural [[catholic:class-thaumaturge]] implement
  (treat as wand or amulet, GM's call).
