# Saint Uriel the Archangel

The angel of fire and revelation, named in 2 Esdras and central to the
1 Enoch tradition where he warns Noah of the coming flood and is set
"over the world and over Tartarus" (1 Enoch 20:2). Catholic devotion
has long received him — Eastern Catholic icons place him among the
seven who stand before the throne — though the 2001 *Directory on
Popular Piety* (§217) reminds the faithful that the Roman Rite names
only the three scriptural archangels in liturgy. Uriel survives in
popular devotion and in Anglican-aligned uses; this lens includes him
as a permitted patron with a flag.

## Cleric / Oracle patron

Cleric of Uriel chooses the **Fire** or **Truth** domain. Oracle takes
the **Cosmos** or **Battle** mystery — the warning-of-judgment +
flame-of-revelation combination is a strong cursebound fit.

- **Edicts (additional)**: warn the unwary of coming judgment, illumine
  what is hidden, witness against falsehood.
- **Anathema (additional)**: hide a true vision, profit from the
  ignorance of another, allow a covenant to be desecrated unchecked.

## Mechanical hooks

- *Holy Light* and *Sunburst* are Uriel's flame on the page.
- *Foresight* is the canonical Uriel-aspected vision spell.
