# Mary, Queen of Heaven — the Theotokos

Mother of God (Theotokos, Council of Ephesus 431), conceived without
original sin (Immaculate Conception, 1854), assumed body and soul into
heaven at her dormition (Assumption, 1950), reigning as Queen of
Heaven and Mother of the Church. The Catholic lens treats her with
*hyperdulia* — veneration above other saints — but never *latria*,
which belongs to God alone. She is the great intercessor and the
Woman of Revelation 12 crowned with twelve stars.

## Champion / Cleric patron

Champion takes cause **Mercy/Redemption** (redeemer) or **Liberation**
(*Magnificat* 1:52, "he has put down the mighty from their thrones").
Cleric chooses the **Family** or **Healing** domain. The Marian
patronage is primarily intercessory — players flavor their
[[catholic:yhwh]]-given graces as Mary-mediated.

- **Edicts (additional)**: do whatever he tells you (Jn 2:5), defend
  the unborn and the lowly, pray for sinners at the hour of death.
- **Anathema (additional)**: despair of mercy, blaspheme the
  Theotokos, abandon a child or mother in distress.

## Mechanical hooks

- *Bless*, *Sanctuary*, and *Calm* (the Remaster *Calm Emotions*) are
  her signature spells; the Magnificat is the in-fiction prayer.
- The Rosary is a meditative practice, not a casting requirement —
  flavor only, no DragonRaid trap.
