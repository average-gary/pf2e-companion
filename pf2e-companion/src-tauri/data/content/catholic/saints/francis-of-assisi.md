# Saint Francis of Assisi

The *Poverello* — little poor man — of Assisi (1181/82–1226). Founder
of the Order of Friars Minor; the first recorded recipient of the
stigmata (La Verna, 1224); composer of the *Canticle of the Sun*
("Brother Sun, Sister Moon"). His radical embrace of poverty and his
preaching to the birds and the wolf of Gubbio make him the great
medieval witness to the kinship of all creation under Christ. Patron
of animals, ecology, merchants (his original family trade), and Italy.

## Champion / Cleric patron

Champion takes cause **Mercy** or **Redemption** — the kiss of the
leper, the embrace of poverty, the disarming of the wolf. Cleric
chooses the **Creation** domain (uniquely well-fitting here) or
**Soul**.

- **Edicts (additional)**: greet every creature as brother or sister,
  embrace voluntary poverty, preach the gospel always — use words when
  necessary.
- **Anathema (additional)**: hoard wealth while others starve, harm
  an animal needlessly, return violence for violence.

## Mechanical hooks

- *Speak with Animals* and *Calm Animals* are flavor-canonical
  Franciscan rituals.
- The stigmata are a high-tier Oracle Curse manifestation — handled
  with reverence; opt-in only.
- The Canticle of the Sun is a textbook Bardic composition reskin.
