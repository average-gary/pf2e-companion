# Saint Michael the Archangel

Prince of the heavenly host. In Daniel "one of the chief princes" who
stands for the people of God; in Jude he disputes with the Devil over
the body of Moses; in Revelation 12:7 he leads the armies of heaven
against the dragon. The Catholic tradition names him patron of the
universal Church, of soldiers, police, and the dying — and prays the
Leonine prayer composed by Leo XIII after his 1884 vision: "Sancte
Michaël Archangele, defende nos in proelio."

## Champion patron

Cause **Justice** (paladin) or **Liberation** (liberator). Edicts and
anathema overlay on the [[catholic:yhwh]] base:

- **Edicts (additional)**: defend the Church against the dragon's
  contracts, contend for the souls of the dying, lead by example in
  spiritual combat.
- **Anathema (additional)**: cede ground to demons by silence or fear,
  break ranks with one's brothers in arms, refuse the Leonine prayer
  when asked.

## Mechanical hooks

- Champion's Aura takes a martial valence — render as the Archangel's
  banner. *Holy Light* (champion feat) reskinned as Michael's spear.
- Michaelmas (29 September) and the Feast of the Archangels are
  natural in-game festival hooks; the Leonine prayer is recommended
  *flavor* only — no real-world recitation required at the table.
