# Saint Thomas the Apostle

Called Didymus, "the Twin." The apostle of doubt-resolved-into-confession:
"Unless I see in his hands the print of the nails... I will not
believe" (Jn 20:25), and then, eight days later, "My Lord and my God"
(Jn 20:28) — the most explicit deity-confession in the gospels.
Tradition sends him to India, where the Mar Thoma Christians of
Kerala still trace their lineage to him; he is martyred there at
Mylapore.

## Champion / Oracle patron

Champion takes cause **Justice** (the doubt-then-confess Champion
fits well) or **Liberation**. Oracle takes the **Lore** or **Battle**
mystery — Thomas's "let us also go, that we may die with him" (Jn
11:16) is courage under existential weight.

- **Edicts (additional)**: examine the wounds before confessing the
  glory, bring the gospel to the ends of the earth, say "my Lord and
  my God" with one's whole heart.
- **Anathema (additional)**: confess what you have not encountered,
  shame the doubter rather than show them the wounds.

## Mechanical hooks

- *True Seeing* (the wound-touching trope) and *Identify* fit the
  Thomistic verification-then-confession pattern.
- Indian-Subcontinent campaign hooks unlock through Thomas's mission.
