# YHWH — the Holy Trinity, Lord of Hosts

The one God in three Persons of Catholic confession: Father, Son, and
Holy Spirit; maker of heaven and earth, covenant-keeper, the I AM of
Exodus 3, the Word made flesh and truly present in the Eucharist, the
Spirit poured out on the Church at Pentecost. The Catholic lens commits
to specifics the [[catholic:yhwh]] entry's Lewisian sibling defers:
seven sacraments as ritual mechanics, Marian theology
([[catholic:mary-queen-of-heaven]]), the communion of saints
([[catholic:cosmology-purgatory]]), the full nine-choir hierarchy of
angels per Aquinas's *Summa* I q.108, and continuationist charisms (1
Cor 12 still active in the Church).

## Stat block

- **Edicts**: love God with all your heart, love your neighbor as
  yourself, honor the sacraments, defend the Church and the poor
- **Anathema**: deny the Real Presence, take innocent life, abandon
  the sacraments, despair of God's mercy
- **Areas of Concern**: covenant, sacraments, the Church, justice,
  mercy
- **Divine Attribute**: Wisdom or Charisma
- **Divine Font**: heal
- **Sanctification**: can choose holy
- **Divine Skill**: Religion
- **Favored Weapon**: longsword (the "sword of the Spirit" — Eph 6:17,
  Heb 4:12)
- **Domains**: Creation, Truth, Healing, Family
- **Alternate Domains**: Soul, Repose, Zeal, Indulgence
- **Cleric Spells**: 1st *bless*; 3rd *heal*; 5th *angelic messenger*
- **Religious Symbol**: Crucifix
- **Sacred Animal**: lamb
- **Sacred Colors**: gold and white

## The Trinity in one entry

Following the Remaster discipline of one-entry-per-deity, the three
Persons appear as in-fiction depth, not as separate stat blocks. The
Father is the source; the Son is the Word made flesh and present in
every Mass; the Holy Spirit is the giver of charisms and indweller of
the baptized. Champions of YHWH may flavor their Spirit's anointing
(the Champion's Aura), the Eucharistic anchor of their power, or a
Marian-mediated grace as the active Person — all are the one God.

## Sacraments as ritual mechanics

The seven sacraments — Baptism, Confirmation, Eucharist, Penance,
Anointing of the Sick, Holy Orders, Matrimony — fit cleanly into the
PF2e ritual subsystem as in-fiction practices clergy NPCs perform.
Sacramentals (blessings, holy water, scapulars, the rosary) are the
broader category and are the natural flavor for [[catholic:class-thaumaturge]]
implements.

## Marian devotion

Mary is venerated (*hyperdulia*), not worshipped (*latria*); see
[[catholic:mary-queen-of-heaven]]. Champions and Clerics may take her
as patron alongside YHWH; she is intercessor and Queen of Heaven, not
deity.

## Pharasma escape hatch

A campaign that prefers not to mechanize divine sanctity at all may set
sanctification to **none** (a real Paizo precedent — Pharasma, *Lost
Omens: Divine Mysteries* 2024). Christian-themed Champions then attach
to a saint, an archangel, or "the cause of righteousness" abstracted,
rather than to YHWH directly.
